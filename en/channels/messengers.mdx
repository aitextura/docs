To have your AI Employee reply to customers in WhatsApp, VK, Instagram, Avito, and other channels, use an **aggregator platform**. An aggregator combines several messengers in one window and provides a single API key for connecting to AI Textura.

<Note>
  In AI Textura, **WhatsApp** connects **only** through aggregators, and **Wazzup** is the most stable option. The platform has no direct WhatsApp channel.
</Note>

## Platform overview

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Wazzup: the WhatsApp specialist">
    Wazzup specializes in connecting WhatsApp (personal number and Business API). It also supports Instagram, Avito, and Telegram. Setup is simple and the connection is stable.

    **Supported channels:** WhatsApp (personal and Business API), Instagram, Avito, Telegram.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Umnico: a universal messenger aggregator">
    Umnico combines WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, VK, Viber, Facebook, Avito, and other channels in one window. It suits companies that need to connect several messengers at once without a CRM.

    **Supported channels:** WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, VK, Viber, Facebook, Avito, and more.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Kommo: a CRM with built-in messengers">
    Kommo (the international version of amoCRM) is a CRM system with native messenger integration. If you already use Kommo for sales, connecting channels is as easy as it gets.

    **Supported channels:** WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, VK.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Salebot: automation and bot builder">
    Salebot is an automation platform with a visual bot builder. It lets you build conversation flows and connect messengers to AI Textura.

    **Supported channels:** WhatsApp, Telegram, VK, Viber.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SendAPI: a universal API for custom integrations">
    SendAPI provides a universal HTTP interface (webhooks) for connecting any channel. It's a good fit if you need a non-standard integration or a channel other aggregators don't offer.

    **Supported channels:** any channel via HTTP webhooks.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Jivo: live chat and omnichannel">
    Jivo offers a live chat widget for websites plus a messenger aggregator. It's convenient if you already run Jivo as live chat on your site: your AI Employee joins the same account.

    **Supported channels:** WhatsApp, VK, Instagram, Telegram, email, website widget.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="HelpDeskEddy: a helpdesk with messengers">
    HelpDeskEddy is a ticket system with messenger support. It suits companies that handle support through tickets and want to add an AI Employee as the first line.

    **Supported channels:** WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, email, tickets.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How to connect

The process is the same for all aggregator platforms.

### Step 1. Set up the aggregator

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up on the aggregator platform">
    Go to the website of your chosen aggregator (Wazzup, Umnico, Kommo, Salebot, SendAPI, Jivo, or HelpDeskEddy) and create an account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the messengers you need">
    In the aggregator's dashboard, add the channels you want to use: WhatsApp, VK, Instagram, Avito, Viber, and others.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the API key">
    Find the **API** or **Integrations** section in the aggregator's settings and copy the API key (token).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Keep your API key secure: it grants full access to your channels in the aggregator. Don't share it with third parties.
</Warning>

### Step 2. Connect the aggregator in AI Textura

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Channel section">
    Go to [AI Employees](/en/guides/ai-employees) → select an AI Employee → **Channel** tab → **Connect New** subtab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a platform">
    Find the one you need in the platform list (for example, Wazzup or Umnico) and click it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a connection">
    In the side panel, select the **Create** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the settings">
    - **Channel Name**: any name that works for you (for example, "WhatsApp via Wazzup").
    - **API Key**: paste the key you copied from the aggregator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect**. The channel appears on the **Connected** tab and starts receiving messages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a source (if applicable)">
    For **Wazzup** and some other aggregators, after connecting you need to choose a specific source channel (for example, the right WhatsApp number) from those available in the aggregator.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can connect several messengers through one aggregator with a single API key.
</Tip>

## Which aggregator to choose

<Note>
  The choice depends on your goals and the channels you want to connect.
</Note>

| Situation | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| WhatsApp is your main channel | **Wazzup**: stable WhatsApp connection, simple setup |
| You need several messengers without a CRM | **Umnico**: the widest channel coverage in one window |
| You already use Kommo CRM | **Kommo**: messengers are built into the CRM, no separate aggregator needed |
| You need automation and bot flows | **Salebot**: visual builder + channel connections |
| You already have live chat on your website | **Jivo**: widget + messengers in one account |
| You handle support through tickets | **HelpDeskEddy**: add AI as the first line |
| Custom integration or a rare channel | **SendAPI**: universal API via webhooks |

<Tip>
  If you need WhatsApp, Instagram, VK, Avito, or other messengers but don't use a CRM, an aggregator (Wazzup or Umnico) is the simplest solution.
</Tip>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I connect several aggregators at once?">
    Yes. You can connect different channels through different aggregators to one AI Employee.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I connect WhatsApp directly, without an aggregator?">
    No. AI Textura has no direct WhatsApp channel. Use Wazzup, Umnico, Kommo, SendAPI, Jivo, or HelpDeskEddy; each of them supports WhatsApp.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if the aggregator goes down?">
    Your AI Employee can't receive or send messages through that channel until the aggregator is back up. Messages customers send during that time will be processed after recovery (depending on the aggregator).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a separate aggregator subscription?">
    Yes. Each aggregator is a separate service with its own pricing. Your AI Textura subscription doesn't include the aggregator's cost.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Avito without an aggregator?">
    AI Textura currently has no direct Avito channel. Avito connects through aggregators that support it (Wazzup, Umnico, SendAPI).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will regular WhatsApp work, or do I need WhatsApp Business?">
    You need the official **WhatsApp Business API**, via 360dialog or an aggregator. A regular account isn't designed for auto-replies: WhatsApp treats this traffic as bulk messaging and restricts the number.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How to avoid losing your WhatsApp number">
    Three rules: connect only through the official Business API; reply to incoming messages and don't message unknown contacts first; don't reply instantly (the message buffer in the channel settings sets the delay).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the phone have to stay on all the time?">
    Only while connecting, for authorization. After that, messages travel between servers, and the phone is only needed to keep the session active.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Instagram: the AI Employee duplicates replies or goes silent on the first message">
    Instagram's built-in auto-replies are to blame: they intercept the first message. Open **Business tools → Automated responses**, turn off welcome messages, then reconnect the channel.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can the AI Employee reply to Instagram comments?">
    Yes, but the aggregator splits comments and direct messages into separate channels, and each channel gets its own AI Employee, since the tone in DMs and under posts differs.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I connected VK, but messages don't arrive">
    Check whether the aggregator channel appears on the AI Employee's **Connected** tab. If it doesn't, the group isn't authorized on the aggregator's side: go back to that step and send a test message again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How to add chat to your website">
    The widget comes from the aggregator or CRM: take the ready-made JS code from your Umnico or amoCRM account or from Bitrix24 Open Channels, and paste it before the closing `</body>` tag on every page of your site. You connect to AI TEXTURA the channel the widget runs through.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Messages from the aggregator stopped arriving">
    The first fix is to reconnect: turn off the channel toggle in the AI Employee's settings, wait 10 seconds, turn it back on, then send a test message. If it's still silent, contact [Support](/en/guides/support) with the aggregator's name and the time of the last message that came through.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Two numbers: can I assign them to two AI Employees?">
    Yes. Each number connects as its own channel, and each channel is linked to one AI Employee.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Should I connect the AI Employee to email?">
    Look at the incoming traffic: if the inbox is mostly automated notifications and newsletters, the AI Employee will be replying to them for nothing. Connect email when it receives real customer inquiries.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
