Telegram is the simplest free way to connect your AI Employee to customers. Conversations run through a Telegram bot: the platform creates one for you or accepts your own, and later you can move that same bot to your personal account so the AI Employee replies on your behalf.

<Note>This page covers private chats. A bot in a group chat is configured differently: see [Telegram Groups](/en/channels/telegram-groups).</Note>

## Connect a bot

Open the AI Employee → **Channel** tab → **Connect New** → **Telegram**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/telegram_connect.png" alt="The Add Channel panel: channel type, Telegram platform, channel name, and the Access block with the Create with Us and Connect Your Own options" />
</Frame>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Create with us">
    Leave **Create with Us** selected and click the blue **Create Telegram Bot** button. The platform opens Telegram and suggests a name and `@username` for you, so you don't need to enter anything, and the bot comes back to AI TEXTURA ready to go.

    You can change the name and look later: the created bot has a **Configure** button.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Connect your own">
    If you already have a bot, select **Connect Your Own** and paste its token into the **Bot Token** field. The AI Employee will start replying through it.

    You get the token from **@BotFather**: the `/mybots` command → your bot → **API Token**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Fill in **Channel Name**. Only you see it; it helps you tell connections apart in the list. Click **Create**: the channel appears on the **Connected** tab and starts receiving messages.

## Replies from your account

Your AI Employee can reply from your personal or work account instead of the bot. The customer sees a familiar profile and can't tell they're talking to AI. The bot stays in place and works as the engine behind the account.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="With Telegram Premium">
    The connection is free and done on your phone: **Settings** → **Telegram Business** → **Chatbots** → paste your bot's address. After that, the AI Employee replies through the bot, but inside your account.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Without Telegram Premium">
    Telegram only offers business mode with a subscription. Without one, connect your account through a [channel manager](/en/channels/messengers) such as Wazzup or Umnico: they pick up messages from your Telegram and pass them to the AI Employee.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Channel settings

These expand at the bottom of the same panel when you create a channel, and you can open them later by clicking the channel on the **Connected** tab.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/telegram_settings.png" alt="The Settings block: AI inline buttons, message buffer with buffer time, typing indicator with delay, and history auto-cleanup" />
</Frame>

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **AI Inline Buttons** | Besides replying in text, the AI Employee adds buttons with options below its reply. Customers find it easier to respond and conversion goes up, but it's obvious a human isn't replying |
| **Message Buffer** | The AI Employee waits before replying, since people often split one question into several messages. **Buffer Time** ranges from a few seconds to 2 minutes; 8–16 seconds works well |
| **Typing Indicator** | Shows a "typing" status in the chat. **Delay** sets when it appears, **Show During Buffer** sets whether it shows while the AI Employee waits for more messages, and **Repeat While AI Is Working** keeps the status on until the reply is sent |
| **History Auto-Cleanup** | Deletes the conversation on a schedule, for example once a day |

<Warning>Auto-cleanup deletes messages permanently. Before turning it on, make sure important information is saved to [Profiles](/en/guides/profiles) or your CRM.</Warning>

## Voice messages

Telegram is the only channel where the AI Employee understands voice messages: it transcribes them and replies in text. If text-to-speech is enabled in the AI Employee's settings, the reply also comes as a voice message.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How to rename the bot and change its avatar">
    You change the display name and picture in **@BotFather** with the `/setname` and `/setuserpic` commands. The `@username`, however, is permanent in Telegram: for a new handle, you'll need to create another bot and connect it in place of the old one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the bot's username have to end in bot">
    Yes, this is a Telegram requirement, not a platform one: `ReveltonAssistantBot` works, `RevAssistant` doesn't. Keep this in mind when choosing a name in @BotFather.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="There's a bot label under the bot's name. Can I remove it?">
    No. Telegram adds this label to every bot without exception. If you want a profile your customers are used to, connect replies from your account through Telegram Business.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I connect one bot to two AI Employees">
    No, a channel is linked to one AI Employee. If you need two, create a second bot in @BotFather and connect it as a separate channel.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The AI Employee replies in groups, but I only want it in private messages">
    The bot has been added to groups, and they come through the same channel. Turn off processing for those conversations in [Chats](/en/guides/chats), or remove the bot from the groups. For how a bot works in a group chat, see [Telegram Groups](/en/channels/telegram-groups).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="People find the test bot in search, and customers message it by mistake">
    Change its display name to something neutral and remove the branded avatar in @BotFather (`/setname`, `/setuserpic`). The bot will no longer show up when people search for your company name, and you can open it via a direct link.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
