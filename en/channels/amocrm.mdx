Customers write in WhatsApp, Telegram, Avito, and VK, and your manager sorts it all out in amoCRM. The **amoCRM** channel routes those same messages to your AI Employee: it replies right in the deal card, the conversation stays where it is, and nobody has to learn a new interface.

## Connect the channel

Open the AI Employee → **Channel** tab → **Connect New** → **amoCRM**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/public/screenshots/31-channel-amocrm.png" alt="The Add Channel panel with the amoCRM platform selected and the CONNECT AMOCRM button" />
</Frame>

Enter a channel name (only you see it) and click **CONNECT AMOCRM**. An amoCRM window opens: sign in to your account and grant access. The platform handles the rest, and the channel appears on the **Connected** tab.

## Choose sources

To open a connected channel, click it on the **Connected** tab. The first thing to do is narrow the scope: your AI Employee doesn't always need to reply in every chat at once.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/amocrm_channel_sources.png" alt="The Edit Channel window: channel name and the expanded Sources list with checkboxes for connected messengers" />
</Frame>

The **Sources** list shows the messengers set up in your amoCRM: Telegram, Avito, VK, Facebook, and others. Uncheck the ones the AI Employee shouldn't handle. For example, leave it Avito and Telegram, and let your managers reply in VK themselves.

**Pipeline Filter** does the same for pipelines. While nothing is selected, the AI Employee replies to all deals. Select one pipeline, and it works only there, leaving your cold leads or post-sale support alone.

## Co-Pilot mode

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/amocrm_copilot.png" alt="The Co-Pilot Mode toggle with a warning that AI replies are posted as notes on the deal" />
</Frame>

With **Co-Pilot** on, the AI Employee sends nothing to the customer. Instead, it writes its reply as a note on the deal: the manager reads the suggestion, edits it as they see fit, and sends it themselves.

This is the best way to roll out an AI Employee in a live sales team: for a week it writes notes, you review what it suggests and refine the [Persona](/en/guides/persona), and only then do you let it talk to customers.

## Settings

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/amocrm_settings.png" alt="The Pipeline Filter and Settings blocks: message buffer with buffer time and history auto-cleanup" />
</Frame>

**Message Buffer** makes the AI Employee wait before replying, since customers often split one thought into several messages. In a CRM, set a longer buffer than in a messenger, up to 120 seconds: messages arrive here with a delay, and an extra minute of silence doesn't bother anyone.

**History Auto-Cleanup** deletes the conversation on a schedule.

<Warning>Auto-cleanup deletes messages permanently.</Warning>

## Actions in amoCRM

The channel only brings in messages; on its own, it doesn't create deals or tasks. To let your AI Employee work with cards hands-on (create deals and contacts, look up customers, add notes and tasks), connect the amoCRM talent on the **Talents** tab: [here's how](/en/guides/connect-talents#how-to-connect-amocrm).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The AI Employee writes to customers on behalf of a real manager">
    The channel is connected under that manager's user account. Create a separate user in amoCRM, such as "AI TEXTURA," and reconnect the channel under it: from then on, replies will go out under that name.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Connecting says the account is already connected, but there's no channel">
    Webhooks from a previous connection are still there. In amoCRM, delete the AI TEXTURA integration entirely along with its webhooks, then delete the channel on the platform and connect again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Conversations in amoCRM close on their own">
    First, check the robots and automation rules in amoCRM itself: they're more often what closes the deal, not the AI Employee. If there are no such rules, contact [Support](/en/guides/support) and include an example deal.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
