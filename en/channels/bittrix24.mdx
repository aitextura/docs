Customers write in WhatsApp, Telegram, Avito, and your website chat, and your managers sort it all out in Bitrix24 Open Channels. The **Bitrix24** channel routes those same messages to your AI Employee: it replies right in the Open Channel, conversations and cards stay where they are, and nobody has to learn a new interface.

## What you need

- Bitrix24 portal administrator rights: only an administrator can access webhooks and Open Channel settings.
- At least one channel connected in the Bitrix24 **Contact Center**, such as Telegram, WhatsApp, Avito, or website live chat. Your AI Employee hears the customer through the Open Channel, not directly.

## Connection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a webhook in Bitrix24">
    Open **Applications → Developer resources → Other → Inbound webhook**. In the **Permissions** block, select: CRM (`crm`), Drive (`disk`), Chatbot creation and management (`imbot`), Chat and notifications (`im`), Users (`user`), Tasks (`task`), Open Channels (`imopenlines`).

    <Frame>
      <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/bitrix24_webhook_rights.png" alt="Creating an inbound webhook in Bitrix24: the URL field and the Permissions block with seven permissions selected" />
    </Frame>

    Click **Create** and copy the **Webhook to call REST API**, an address like `https://your-portal.bitrix24.ru/rest/1/abcdef0123456/`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go back to AI TEXTURA and connect the channel">
    Open the AI Employee → **Channel** tab → **Connect New** → **Bitrix24**. Enter a **Channel Name** (only you see it), paste the webhook address into the **Webhook URL** field, and click **Create**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/bitrix24_channel_connect.png" alt="The Add Channel panel with the Bitrix24 platform: channel name, Webhook URL field, and message buffer settings" />
    </Frame>

    The channel appears on the **Connected** tab, and the platform creates a chatbot in Bitrix24: you can see its number in the **bot_id** field inside the channel.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go back to Bitrix24 and assign the chatbot">
    Until the chatbot is assigned as the responsible person, messages from the Open Channel don't reach your AI Employee.

    Open the **Contact Center** and select the channel where the AI Employee should reply, such as Telegram, WhatsApp, or website live chat.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/bitrix24_contact_center.png" alt="Bitrix24 Contact Center: channel tiles for telephony, website widget and form, live chat, WhatsApp, Telegram, VK" />
    </Frame>

    In the channel settings, open the **Chatbots** section, turn on **Assign chatbot as responsible person when a customer makes contact**, and select the bot that appeared after you connected the channel.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/bitrix24_chatbot.png" alt="Open Channel settings in Bitrix24, Chatbots section: the checkbox for assigning the bot as responsible and the bot selection list" />
    </Frame>

    | Field | What to set |
    |---|---|
    | **When to connect the chatbot** | At the start of every new conversation |
    | **When to transfer the conversation from the chatbot to the queue** | Don't transfer; otherwise Bitrix24 will take the conversation away from the AI Employee and hand it to the managers' queue |
    | **When to disconnect the chatbot** | After the conversation ends |

    Save the Open Channel settings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go back to AI TEXTURA and configure the channel">
    Open the channel by clicking it on the **Connected** tab and go through the settings: pipeline filter, operator involvement, message buffer, Co-Pilot mode.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Test it

Write to the connected channel as a customer, for example to the Telegram bot set up in this Open Channel. Your AI Employee replies in the same chat, and the conversation appears in [Chats](/en/guides/chats).

## Channel settings

The defaults don't suit every portal, so go through the settings right away.

**Pipeline Filter** narrows the scope. While nothing is selected, the AI Employee replies to all deals. Select one pipeline, and it works only there, leaving your cold leads or post-sale support alone.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/bitrix24_funnels.png" alt="The Pipeline Filter block with the hint: if nothing is selected, the AI replies to all deals" />
</Frame>

**Connect Operators in Parallel** adds the selected managers to the conversation after the AI Employee replies, so they can see the conversation and step in. The **Forced** toggle adds them on every incoming message without waiting for a reply.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/bitrix24_operators.png" alt="The Connect Operators in Parallel and Forced toggles in the channel settings" />
</Frame>

**Message Buffer** makes the AI Employee wait before replying, since customers often split one thought into several messages. The default **Buffer Time** is 8 seconds; in Open Channels, set it longer, up to 120 seconds, because messages arrive with a delay.

**History Auto-Cleanup** deletes the conversation on a schedule.

<Warning>Auto-cleanup deletes messages permanently.</Warning>

## Co-Pilot mode

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/bitrix24_copilot.png" alt="The Co-Pilot Mode toggle: the AI replies with notes for the manager instead of messages to the customer" />
</Frame>

With **Co-Pilot** on, the AI Employee sends nothing to the customer. Instead, it writes its reply as a note for the manager, who reads the suggestion, edits it as they see fit, and sends it themselves.

This is the best way to roll out an AI Employee in a live sales team: for a week it writes notes, you review what it suggests and refine the [Persona](/en/guides/persona), and only then do you let it talk to customers.

## Actions in Bitrix24

The channel only brings in messages: it doesn't create deals or leads, set tasks, or assign responsible persons. The Bitrix24 talent does all that: [how to connect it](/en/guides/connect-talents#how-to-connect-bitrix24).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which Bitrix24 plan do I need">
    The Basic plan is enough: you need Open Channels and the Developer resources section with inbound webhooks, and both are included in Basic.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A customer sent a photo. Will it reach the AI Employee?">
    Open Channels pass attachments as links, and how a file looks in the portal depends on the message source. Test it on your setup: send a test photo from WhatsApp, Telegram, and the website widget.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Notifications in Bitrix24

You can receive alerts that a customer is asking for a manager in the portal chat, sent to specific employees, instead of in Telegram. Set this up on the [Notifications](/en/guides/notifications) page.
