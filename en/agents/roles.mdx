A role saves you from a blank page: the platform writes the agent's personality, workflow, and first questions for you. You pick it on the second wizard step, **"Where do we start?"**. Six cards are shown by default; the **Show more** link expands all 25.

## Personal and organization

| Role | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Personal Assistant** | Handles your affairs: tasks, agreements, drafts |
| **Morning Briefing** | Sends a short brief every morning: the day's plan, deadlines, and loose ends |
| **Second Brain** | A knowledge vault: you send links, articles, and thoughts — it files them in the right place |
| **Secretary** | Meeting minutes, business emails, tasks, and contacts |
| **Meeting Minutes Taker** | Turns meeting notes and transcripts into clear minutes |
| **Finance Assistant** | Tracks expenses and income, budgets, and gives honest summaries of where the money went |
| **Habit Coach** | Morning checklist, evening review, and tracking of what's sticking |

## Content and social media

| Role | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Copywriter** | Writes posts, emails, and landing pages in your voice |
| **SMM Manager** | Runs the content plan: ideas, posts, and captions for your platforms |
| **Content Factory** | A publishing pipeline: keeps the calendar and puts out content in a steady stream |
| **TikTok Factory** | Short videos: ideas, hooks, and second-by-second scripts |

## Customers and sales

| Role | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sales Manager** | Customer emails, scripts, objection handling, and follow-ups |
| **Customer Onboarding** | Guides each new customer through the launch stages: welcomes, checklists, reminders |
| **Review Replies** | Drafts replies to reviews — from thank-yous to handling complaints |
| **Customer Support** | Answers from your materials and hands complex cases to a human |
| **Meeting Dossier** | Prepares you for negotiations: compiles a dossier on the person and the company |

<Note>The **Customer Support** role answers you, not your customers directly. For live conversations with customers in channels, you need an [AI Employee](/en/guides/ai-employees) — it has a Knowledge Base, Self-Learning, and Working Hours.</Note>

## Data and monitoring

| Role | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Researcher** | Gathers information and turns it into short summaries |
| **Report Analyst** | You send exports and numbers — you get a clear report |
| **Trend Radar** | Scans sources in your niche every morning and sends a digest |
| **Mention Radar** | Tracks what people say about your brand and competitors |
| **Price Radar** | Tracks competitor prices on specific pages and keeps a history |

## Documents and learning

| Role | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Automator** | Turns routine work into scripts in its own workspace |
| **Contract Review** | Reads contracts for you and translates legalese into plain language |
| **Recruiter** | Writes job postings and compares résumés against requirements |
| **Tutor** | Explains things in simple terms, keeps a study plan, and tracks progress |

## How to choose

Pick a role by the action, not the job title. Need a regular report — **Report Analyst**. Need file processing and scripts — **Automator**. Need one short text every morning — **Morning Briefing**.

A role doesn't restrict anything: it sets the starting personality text and the first conversation. An agent with the Tutor role won't refuse to tally your expenses — it'll just do it in a tutor's manner, and worse than an agent set up for that.

<Tip>The **From scratch** link below the list creates an agent with no personality. This only makes sense if you're ready to write the **Soul** document yourself — otherwise the agent will ask follow-up questions at every step. You can always edit the written personality: [Personality](/en/agents/personality).</Tip>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the role of an existing agent?">
    There's no separate "change role" button — the role becomes text on the **Personality** tab. Open the **Soul** document and rewrite it for the new task: that's how you change the role. While you're at it, clean up **Long-Term Memory** if the agent has already picked up facts from its previous work.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can one agent have two roles?">
    Technically yes, practically it works poorly. Working memory is finite: an agent with two areas of responsibility starts mixing up contexts and ignoring its own rules. It's better to deploy a second agent (you're allowed two) or add a [persona](/en/agents/team) to the current one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="None of the roles fits">
    Take the closest one by type of action and rewrite the **Soul** in your own words. That's faster and more reliable than creating an agent **From scratch**: a ready-made role already has working habits built in — keeping a log, maintaining a task list, asking questions one at a time.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
