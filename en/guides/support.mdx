Support is a built-in request (ticket) system for contacting the AI Textura team. Here you can ask a question, report a problem, or get help with setup -- and receive a reply right inside the platform, without switching to email or messengers.

Besides regular tickets, you have two "fast lanes" to support:
- **The thumbs-down button** on any AI reply -- starts a [4-step review](#how-to-report-an-ai-reply-thumbs-down) that creates a ticket automatically.
- **The Support access to account toggle** -- lets the team look at your chats, agents, and settings so they can solve the problem faster.

## Support access to your account

At the top of the **Support** page is the **Allow support to access account** toggle (new in v2.5.0). When it's on, the status **Access granted** appears next to it.

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Access granted** | The AI Textura support team can sign in to your account on your behalf to look at [chats](/en/guides/chats), [agent](/en/guides/ai-employees) settings, [talents](/en/guides/talents), the [Knowledge Base](/en/guides/knowledge), and other sections -- to diagnose the problem |
| **Access denied** | Support sees only your ticket text and attached files -- with no way to sign in to your account |

<Tip>
  If your ticket is about agent setup, AI behavior in a specific chat, or connecting a channel, **turn on access before sending the ticket**. This speeds things up considerably: the team doesn't need to ask you for screenshots and videos -- they'll look for themselves.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Access granted stays in effect until you turn the toggle off yourself. Once the problem is solved, we recommend turning access off again. On critical accounts with financial operations, turn on access only while a specific ticket is being investigated.
</Warning>

## How to report an AI reply (thumbs down)

If an AI Employee gave a customer a wrong reply, you don't need to create a ticket manually -- the message has a **Mark reply as unhelpful** button (thumbs down). It starts a 4-step review: the platform first tries to explain the cause of the bad reply **on its own**, and if you still have questions, it creates a request to the **AI training team** with an automatically attached "Context report".

### Where to find the button

In the [Chats](/en/guides/chats) section, open any conversation. Every AI Employee message has a thumbs-down button (aria-label: `Mark reply as unhelpful`). Click it to open a review dialog.

### Step 1 of 4 -- Bad reply review

<Frame><img src="/public/screenshots/45-chat-thumbdown-modal.png" alt="Step 1 — automatic diagnostics of a bad reply" /></Frame>

The platform shows **automatic diagnostics** of how the agent arrived at this reply:

| Agent decision step | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Language detection** | Which language was detected in the customer's message (for example, "Message language: English") |
| **Tools used** | An expandable list of [talents](/en/guides/talents) the agent called (`check_language`, `crm_create_lead`, etc.) |
| **What the agent did** | The source of the reply: general knowledge, the [Knowledge Base](/en/guides/knowledge), a [talent](/en/guides/talents), or a combination |
| **Final decision** | The resulting action -- for example, "Nothing was sent to the customer -- the agent stayed silent (strict mode with no relevant sources)" |

At the bottom are three buttons:

| Button | When to choose it |
|---|---|
| **Cancel** | Close the review without sending |
| **It's the settings** | If the diagnostics already show the cause is in the instructions or prompt -- go to step 2 to check |
| **Something else →** | If the diagnostics don't explain the problem -- skip the check and describe it in your own words (step 3) |

<Note>
  If automatic diagnostics aren't available, the platform shows: "Couldn't get automatic diagnostics. Review the timeline below manually." This is rare -- proceed as usual and describe the problem in step 3.
</Note>

### Step 2 of 4 -- Check the instructions and tools

<Frame><img src="/public/screenshots/46-thumbdown-step2.png" alt="Step 2 — checking instructions and tools" /></Frame>

"The instruction may be incomplete -- the agent didn't understand it needed to call a tool." At this step, the platform shows **the list of tools the agent used in this case** (`check_language`, Knowledge Base search, CRM operations, etc.).

You decide whether the [Persona](/en/guides/persona) or the working rules themselves are to blame:

| Button | What it means |
|---|---|
| **← Back** | Return to the automatic diagnostics |
| **In the instructions** | Confirm that the cause is in the agent's instructions -- go to step 3 to report to the training team |
| **Instructions are OK →** | The instructions look fine -- describe the problem separately (step 3) |

<Tip>
  Before clicking **In the instructions**, open the AI Employee's [Persona](/en/guides/persona) in a separate tab and check whether it actually describes how to call the tool in question. Sometimes fixing the prompt is faster than waiting for a support reply.
</Tip>

### Step 3 of 4 -- Describe the problem

<Frame><img src="/public/screenshots/47-thumbdown-step3.png" alt="Step 3 — describing the problem" /></Frame>

Here you fill in three fields -- they make up the body of the ticket:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **What should the reply have said?** *(textarea, required)* | Describe the correct reply or what was missing from the actual one |
| **What do you think caused the error?** *(select)* | Choose one of the categories -- see the table below |
| **Additional comments** *(textarea)* | Any useful information for the training team |

#### Cause options

| Cause | When to choose it |
|---|---|
| **Inaccuracy (made-up facts)** | The AI gave information that doesn't exist in reality or in the Knowledge Base |
| **Misunderstood the question** | The AI answered the "wrong" question -- it misinterpreted the customer |
| **Didn't use knowledge / tool** | The agent has a [Knowledge Base](/en/guides/knowledge) or [talent](/en/guides/talents) but didn't use it |
| **Rudeness / inappropriate tone** | A style problem -- the [tone](/en/guides/persona) or ethics were violated |
| **Too long / too short** | The reply format didn't fit |
| **Other** | Any other situation -- explain in the text fields |

The **Next →** button becomes active only after you fill in the first textarea.

### Step 4 of 4 -- Thank you! Your feedback matters

<Frame><img src="/public/screenshots/48-thumbdown-step4-support.png" alt="Step 4 — final screen" /></Frame>

The final screen gathers everything into one ticket:

- Summary: `Expected reply: {your text from step 3}`, `Cause: {selected cause}`.
- Message: **"This information will go to the AI training team. We use it to make agents better. A reply usually arrives within 3 business days."**
- **Key point**: **"The case context report will be automatically attached to the support request."**
- The **📎 Open context report →** link -- lets you view a detailed report of the agent's decision steps before sending.
- Buttons: `← Back`, `Send`.

After you click **Send**, a new ticket with the **On Customer** status appears in the [Support](/en/guides/support) section -- the AI training team usually gets back to you within 3 business days.

<Note>
  Thumbs down is **not just a rating for analytics**. It's a **ticket format** for a specialized team: it automatically collects all the technical information (message language, [talents](/en/guides/talents) used, [Knowledge Base](/en/guides/knowledge) search results, the agent's final decision) and attaches it to the request. The team doesn't need to ask you for context -- it's already there.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Use thumbs down for any inaccuracy in an AI reply -- even if you're not sure the problem is serious. The more of these signals the training team gets, the faster agents become more accurate.
</Tip>

## How to create a new ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Support section">
    Click **Support** in the platform's left menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click + New ticket">
    The button is at the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a title">
    Briefly describe the essence of the question or problem.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the first message">
    Explain in detail what happened or what you need. Specify which AI Employee or feature it concerns, what you did, and what went wrong.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the ticket">
    Once created, the ticket appears in the request list with the **On Customer** status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

</Tip>

## Messaging inside a ticket

After you create a ticket, the conversation takes place in chat format -- right in the right-hand panel of the Support section. Your messages appear on the right, the team's replies on the left.

To send a message:
1. Open the ticket by clicking it in the list.
2. Type your text in the input field at the bottom.
3. Send the message.

When the team replies, a red indicator with the number of unread messages appears next to the ticket in the list.

<Note>
  If you want to add to your request, just write a new message in the same ticket. You don't need to create a separate request.
</Note>

## Ticket statuses

Each ticket has one of two statuses:

| Status | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **On Customer** | Waiting for your reply or just created | Check whether the team has a question for you -- and answer it |
| **Done** | The request is closed, the issue is resolved | Nothing -- the ticket is archived |

The status updates automatically: when the team replies and waits for your response, the ticket moves to **On Customer**. When the issue is resolved, the team moves the ticket to **Done**.

<Warning>
  A closed ticket can't be reopened. If the problem happens again or you have a new question, create a separate ticket. You can reference the previous request in the text.
</Warning>

## Response times

The AI Textura team tries to respond to requests as quickly as possible. Approximate times:

| Request type | Approximate response time |
|---|---|
| Critical problem (platform down, charges) | Within a few hours |
| Question about setup or features | Within one business day |
| Suggestion or feature request | Within 1–2 business days |

<Tip>
  If your question is urgent, say so in the ticket title. For example: "URGENT: AI Employee stopped replying in Telegram". This helps the team prioritize your request.
</Tip>

## Tips for effective requests

- **One question -- one ticket.** This makes it easier for the team to track and resolve each task.
- **Answer the team's questions** -- it speeds up the resolution.

<Info>
  **To get the fastest response to your request**, attach the following to your ticket:

  1. **A screenshot of the error** -- attach the image right in the message so the team can see what's happening immediately.
  2. **A link to the conversation** -- open the [Chats](/en/guides/chats) section, find the conversation, and copy the URL from your browser's address bar. That way support can see the conversation and understand the situation.
  3. **Steps to reproduce** (if the error is complex) -- describe step by step what to do to reproduce the error. This lets the team repeat the scenario and see in the conversation how it works.
</Info>

**Example of a good request:**
> Title: "Booking link doesn't work"
> Message: "The 'Concierge' AI Employee in Telegram sends customers a booking link, but it opens a blank page. Link: [URL]. The problem started yesterday. Conversation link: https://app.aitextura.com/chats/... To reproduce: write to the bot 'book a room for March 20' -- the reply will contain a broken link."

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I attach a screenshot or file to a ticket?">
    Yes! You can attach images and files right in a ticket message. We recommend always adding a screenshot of the error -- it speeds up the resolution considerably.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I know I've received a reply?">
    A red indicator with the number of unread messages appears next to the ticket in the request list.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I reopen a closed ticket?">
    No. If the issue comes back, create a new ticket. You can reference the previous request in the text.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many tickets can I create?">
    There's no limit. Create as many requests as you need.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who answers the requests?">
    The AI Textura support team -- real people who know the platform inside out. It's not a bot.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is support available 24/7?">
    The team works during business hours (Moscow time), but critical requests may be handled outside the standard schedule.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is thumbs down different from a regular ticket?">
    Thumbs down is a **specialized channel** for requests about AI reply quality. The platform collects all the technical information itself (message language, [talents](/en/guides/talents), [Knowledge Base](/en/guides/knowledge), final decision) and attaches it to the ticket. The request goes to the **AI training team**. A regular ticket is a free-form request to general support: questions about billing, connecting channels, access, and so on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is it safe to turn on Support access to account?">
    Yes. Access is granted only to AI Textura support staff and is used to diagnose your problems. All actions are logged on the platform side. Once the ticket is resolved, we recommend turning the toggle off -- you can do this at any time on the **Support** page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's in the Context report attached via thumbs down?">
    The report includes: the language of the customer's message, the list of [talents](/en/guides/talents) called and their results, what was found in the [Knowledge Base](/en/guides/knowledge), and what final decision the agent made. You can view the report before sending via the "📎 Open context report" link in step 4.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The platform or my account won't open">
    First, check `status.aitextura.com` -- maintenance and outages are posted there, and urgent updates are duplicated in the platform's Telegram channel. If the status is green, try another browser and clear your cache, then write to us.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The AI Employee stopped replying completely -- where do I start">
    In order: your word balance isn't at zero; the AI Employee is turned on and its channel is active; turn the channel off and on again -- this rebuilds the connection; check `status.aitextura.com`. If everything is fine, create a ticket and attach a link to the conversation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="OpenAI is down -- what should we do">
    Nothing. The platform switches to backup models automatically; replies may be slower than usual during that time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which models does the platform run on">
    On models from OpenAI, Google, and other leading providers -- the platform doesn't train its own models. The platform itself is written in NodeJS with Python modules.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where can I watch training videos">
    On the AI TEXTURA YouTube channel. Videos for specific sections are embedded right in the documentation articles -- on the [Persona](/en/guides/persona), [Knowledge Base](/en/guides/knowledge), [Talents](/en/guides/talents), [Notifications](/en/guides/notifications) pages, and others.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
