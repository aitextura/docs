## What broadcasts are

A broadcast sends the same (or a personalized) message to a list of recipients through the channel where they've already talked to you. This is **not** cold outreach -- messages go only to customers who have already had a conversation with your AI Employee and have accepted communication through the channel.

Typical use cases:

- Announcing a promotion or a new service.
- Reminding about an unfinished order or an abandoned cart.
- Re-engaging customers who haven't written in a long time.
- Collecting feedback after a purchase.
- Announcing changes in how your company works (hours, new services).

<Warning>
  Broadcasts depend heavily on the channel: in a Telegram bot, a message reaches only users who started the conversation themselves; in CRM channels, it reaches customers with an open conversation. Follow each platform's policies.
</Warning>

## Where to find it

In the platform sidebar, select **Broadcasts** (URL `/broadcast`). The section is available in any context -- your personal account and an [organization](/en/guides/organization).

---

## How to create a broadcast

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Broadcasts section">
    In the sidebar, click **Broadcasts** (`/broadcast`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new broadcast">
    Click the button to create a new broadcast in the top-right corner of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a channel and segment">
    Specify which connected channel to send the message through and who exactly should get it -- a customer segment or a specific list of recipients.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the message">
    Enter the broadcast text. Use personalization where possible -- addressing the customer by name, linking to a specific product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send or schedule">
    You can send it right away or schedule it for a specific time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it relates to other sections

- **Channels.** A broadcast goes through a channel already connected to one of your AI Employees. See [Channels](/en/channels/index).
- **Customer Profiles.** Segmenting by [Profiles](/en/guides/profiles) lets you write only to the group you need -- for example, only those who made a purchase in the last 30 days.
- **Notifications.** Don't confuse them: [Notifications](/en/guides/notifications) are alerts to your team about events in chats. Broadcasts are messages to your customers.
- **Billing.** Each broadcast message uses words, just like a regular conversation. Keep an eye on your balance -- see [Billing](/en/guides/finance).

## Best practices

<Steps>
  <Step title="Segment your recipients">
    The narrower the segment, the higher the response. Don't send the same message to everyone at once; split your audience by interest, how recent the conversation was, and customer status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use personalization">
    Insert the name, region, or last order. Impersonal "Hello, we have a promotion" messages perform worst.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test on a small group">
    Before a mass send, do a trial run on 10–20 customers and evaluate the response.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Don't send too often">
    A broadcast once a week is normal. Every day is a path to unsubscribes and blocks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who receives a broadcast?">
    Only customers with an active conversation in the selected channel -- that is, those who have already written to your AI Employee. Cold outreach to strangers isn't supported.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I cancel a broadcast that's already running?">
    If a broadcast is scheduled for the future, you can cancel or edit it before it starts. Once sending has begun, you can't fully undo it, but you can stop further sending.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How much does a broadcast cost?">
    Each message sent uses words from your plan -- just like a regular agent reply in a chat. See [Billing](/en/guides/finance).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which channels can I broadcast through?">
    Any channel connected to your AI Employee. Technical limits depend on the specific platform -- for example, in Telegram you can't message first someone who hasn't started a conversation themselves.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I send a broadcast on behalf of an organization instead of my personal account?">
    Yes -- switch the context to the [organization](/en/guides/organization), and that organization's channels and agents become available.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where can I see broadcast statistics?">
    On the broadcast's own page -- sent / read / replied. Customer replies land in the regular chat of the corresponding AI Employee.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
