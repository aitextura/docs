This is where all your AI Employees live: which ones have a channel connected, which one replied a minute ago, and which one has been idle for a month. From here you hire a new AI Employee and open the settings of an existing one.

<Note>In the platform menu, this section is called **Agents**: the list holds both AI Employees and [Autonomous Agents](/en/agents) — a second type that doesn't talk to customers but works for you.</Note>

## Hire an AI Employee

Click **Hire Agent** in the top-right corner. There are three ways:

| Option | What happens | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Manually** | You pick a ready-made template from the library — it comes with instructions already written for a typical task | Anyone getting started |
| **From a website link** | The platform reads the page and builds the first instructions from it: the business, services, tone | If you have a website or landing page describing your services |
| **From scratch** | A blank AI Employee with no instructions at all | Those who know exactly what they want to write |

Right after hiring, the AI Employee editor opens. The first tab you see is [Persona](/en/guides/persona) — start there; the other settings are organized in tabs along the top.

## The list

Click a row to open that AI Employee's settings.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The AI Employee's avatar and name |
| **Status** | **Active** — the AI Employee is working |
| **Created** | When the AI Employee was hired |
| **Channels** | Icons of connected channels. A dash means the AI Employee isn't connected to anything yet and doesn't answer real customers |
| **Talents** | How many talents are connected |
| **Last activity** | When the AI Employee last replied in a conversation |
| **Chats** | The **Live chat** button |
| **Actions** | An on/off toggle and the **...** menu |

The toggle turns the AI Employee off completely: it stops processing new messages, and customers get no reply. If you only want to take it off night shifts, use [Working Hours](/en/guides/working-hours) instead of turning it off.

The **...** menu has three actions: **Edit** opens the settings, **Copy** creates a copy with all settings, and **Delete**.

<Warning>Deletion can't be undone: the AI Employee is removed along with its Persona, knowledge, and connections.</Warning>

## Live chat

The **Live chat** button opens the same test chat you have inside the AI Employee. What makes it useful is that you can copy the link from the address bar and send it to anyone. The other person doesn't need a platform account or a connected channel: they open the link and talk to your AI Employee.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The AI Employee is created but doesn't answer customers">
    Check in this order: the toggle in the **Actions** column is on, at least one [channel](/en/channels/index) is connected, and it isn't a day off according to [Working Hours](/en/guides/working-hours).

    If there are unconfirmed entries on the [Self-Learning](/en/guides/self-learning) tab, chats may be paused — review them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a separate AI Employee for each property or branch?">
    Not necessarily. If your properties follow the same rules, one is enough — describe each property in its own block in the [Persona](/en/guides/persona). Use separate AI Employees when properties have different services, different rules, or their own PMS: otherwise the AI Employee will start mixing their data in a single reply.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="One channel for several branches — the AI Employee mixes up properties">
    The clean solution is a separate channel and a separate AI Employee for each branch. If you have only one channel, add an explicit rule to the [Persona](/en/guides/persona): which property was asked about, and which source to take the data from.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I created an AI Employee from a website link, but it knows very little">
    The platform reads only the page you linked, not the whole website. Check the result in the [Persona](/en/guides/persona) and add the rest to the [Knowledge Base](/en/guides/knowledge).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I hand a conversation over to another AI Employee?">
    Use [Quick Answers](/en/guides/quick-answers): the **Transfer to another AI** action hands over the whole conversation, and **Get answer from another AI** brings a colleague's reply into the current conversation. This is also how you build a setup with a dispatcher AI Employee: it identifies the topic and routes the customer to the right specialist.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A colleague just needs to talk to the AI Employee — do they need an account?">
    No. Send them the **Live chat** link. Only people who will log in to the platform and change settings need an account — add them to the [Organization](/en/guides/organization) with the right role.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many AI Employees can I hire?">
    The limit depends on your plan — you can see the current limits in the [billing section](/en/guides/finance).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
