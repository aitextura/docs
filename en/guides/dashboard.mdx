The **Dashboard** shows the results of your AI Employee's work: how many conversations it handled, how good its replies were, how fast it responds, and how fast a manager steps in. Each widget has its own period switch — **6 months, 3 months, 30 days, 7 days, or 24 hours** — and the percentage next to a number compares it with the previous period of the same length.

## Analytics

Four cards with the key numbers for the period. Click any of them, and the chart below shows its daily trend.

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Cost per message** | The average cost of one reply from your AI Employee |
| **Messages** | How many messages it handled |
| **Chats** | How many conversations it handled |
| **Self-Learning** | How many questions went to [Self-Learning](/en/guides/self-learning) and are waiting for your review |

## AI Quality

A rating of replies for the period: **Quality** as a percentage and **Negative** — how many replies were marked as poor, including with the thumbs-down button in [Chats](/en/guides/chats). The chart next to it shows how quality changed day by day.

## Response Time

This widget compares two speeds: how fast your AI Employee replies and how long it takes a manager to join when they step into a conversation. For the AI Employee it's seconds; for a human it's minutes or hours. The daily chart shows when the gap gets especially wide.

This is also where you see your team's workload: if managers keep getting slower, hand some routine questions over to your AI Employee — through [Quick Answers](/en/guides/quick-answers) or the [Knowledge Base](/en/guides/knowledge).

## AI Efficiency

How much work your AI Employee handles on its own. Shown as a table or a chart, broken down **By agent**, **By channel**, or **By conversation**.

| Column | What it means |
|---|---|
| **AI without manager** | Conversations your AI Employee closed on its own — the main automation metric |
| **Manager only** | Conversations where only a human replied |
| **AI + manager** | Conversations handled by both |

## Spending

The **Spending and Charges Analytics** card leads to [Billing](/en/guides/finance), where you can see how much each AI Employee spent and on which channels. Your current balance is always shown in the top-right corner.
