A customer asked for a manager, started a fight, or agreed to a booking — you want to know right away, not in the evening when you check your chats. **Notifications** send you the gist of the conversation in Telegram, along with a button to jump right into it.

## Subscribe

Open the AI Employee → **Notifications** tab and click **Get notifications**. The platform redirects you to Telegram — tap **Start** there, and all notifications from this employee will come to your phone.

<Frame>
  <img src="/public/screenshots/32-agent-notifications.png" alt="The Notifications tab: the Subscribe to notifications block with the Get notifications button" />
</Frame>

<Tip>To have a colleague receive notifications too, click the same button and copy the link — send it to a manager, administrator, or supervisor. Everyone who follows it and taps **Start** will receive the same notifications.</Tip>

Here's what a notification looks like: the employee's name, the channel, the customer's message, the employee's reply, and the summary you asked for.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/notification_telegram_example.png" alt="A Conflict situation notification in Telegram: employee, channel, customer details, incoming message, employee reply, additional data, and the Open chat button" />
</Frame>

At the bottom is the **Open chat** button: it takes you straight to this conversation, so you don't have to search for it among your chats. One click and you're replying to the customer yourself.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="If you work in Bitrix24">
    You can receive notifications right inside your portal — in a chat with specific staff members, without setting up Telegram.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create a webhook in Bitrix24">
        **Applications → Webhooks → Add webhook → Inbound webhook**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select permissions">
        **Chat and notifications (im)** and **Users (user_basic)**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Paste the URL into AI TEXTURA">
        Copy the webhook URL, expand the **Bitrix24** block on the **Notifications** tab, paste the URL, and click **Load users**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose recipients">
        In the **User IDs** field, select who should receive notifications and click **Save**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="If Telegram doesn't work for you and you don't have Bitrix24">
    That leaves a webhook: notifications are sent to your URL, and from there you route them wherever you like — your own system, email, another messenger. This is a job for a developer.

    Expand the **Notification webhook** block, paste the URL into the **Webhook URL** field, and click **Save**. The platform sends a POST request with JSON: the fields `event`, `version`, `timestamp`, `data`. The **Show webhook format** button shows the exact format.

    An empty field disables the webhook.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Set up a trigger

A trigger is a rule that sends a notification. It isn't set for the employee as a whole but for a channel: in each trigger, you choose which channel's conversations it watches. Until you have triggers, nothing will arrive, even if you've subscribed.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/notifications_triggers_list.png" alt="Trigger templates, the Add new trigger button, and the list of created triggers" />
</Frame>

Pick one of the templates — **Human needed**, **Booking confirmed**, **Agent made a promise** — or click **Add new trigger**. The settings window opens.

<Frame>
  <img src="/public/screenshots/33-notification-add-trigger.png" alt="Trigger settings window: name, direction, working channel, condition, and additional data" />
</Frame>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Name">
    The notification's title — you'll see it when the trigger fires. Make it clear at a glance: "Manager needed in chat," "Send an invoice," "Conflict."
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When to use">
    Describe in your own words the moment you want a notification for. These aren't keywords: a separate AI reads the conversation and decides whether the event has happened.

    > When the customer asks to bring a human into the chat — an operator, manager, or administrator

    > When the customer asks for an invoice
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Additional data in the message">
    Here you list what you want to see in the notification itself: all booking details, full name, order total, a brief summary of the request.

    It works the same way: the AI reads the whole conversation and pulls a short summary from it — only the data you asked for. Without this field, you get the bare fact that the trigger fired, with no context.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Working channel">
    The channel the trigger is tied to: if you choose Telegram, it watches only Telegram conversations. Until a channel is selected, the notification will never arrive.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Incoming and outgoing">
    | Toggle | When it fires |
    |---|---|
    | **Incoming** | The customer sent a message |
    | **Outgoing** | The employee sent a reply |

    This determines how you phrase the condition. For incoming: "when the customer asks for a refund"; for outgoing: "when the employee promised to call back."
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Chat pause">
    Besides sending a notification, a trigger can stop the employee so it doesn't interrupt the manager.

    | Mode | What happens | When to choose it |
    |---|---|---|
    | **Regular pause** | The employee stays silent for 10–20 minutes and returns to the conversation on its own if no manager joins | Informational triggers: booking confirmed, employee made a promise |
    | **Forced pause** | The employee stays silent until a manager turns it back on manually or sends the [“Start AI”](/en/guides/additional-settings) command in the chat | Complaints, refunds, escalation — where the AI shouldn't step in |
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>You can create up to 10 triggers per channel.</Note>

## How to test

Notifications come only from channels — Telegram, WhatsApp, Bitrix24, amoCRM. They don't work in the account's test chat, so test them this way:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect a channel">
    One is enough; the fastest is a [Telegram bot](/en/channels/telegram).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up a trigger for that channel">
    Take the **Human needed** template and select the channel in the **Working channel** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Subscribe">
    Click **Get notifications**, then **Start** in Telegram.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write &quot;I need a manager&quot; to the bot">
    Or take the conversation to the point where the employee promises to pass the question to a human.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive the notification">
    It contains all the important information from the conversation — and a button to open it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The employee wrote &quot;I'll pass this to a manager,&quot; but no notification arrived">
    The employee only said it. That phrase comes from the escalation rules in the [Persona](/en/guides/persona), which control what the customer reads in the chat. Handing the conversation over to you is the trigger's job, and it's a separate feature: until a trigger exists, the employee will keep making empty promises.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Notifications don't arrive in the test chat">
    That's expected: the account's test chat doesn't send them. Test your setup by chatting in a connected channel — for example, a Telegram bot.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The trigger doesn't fire">
    The condition is vague or too narrow. Rewrite it in the first person and in more detail: not "complaint," but "when I complain about service quality or threaten to leave a negative review." Also check that the trigger is active and the right working channel is selected.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Notifications arrive too often">
    The condition is too broad. Add specifics: instead of "when I ask a question," use "when I ask a question the employee couldn't answer and express dissatisfaction."
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The notification is missing data I need">
    Add it to the **Additional data in the message** field: name, booking number, total, the gist of the request. The AI includes only what you listed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get notifications by email?">
    Not directly. With a webhook and an automation service like Make, n8n, or Zapier, you can set up email in a few minutes.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
