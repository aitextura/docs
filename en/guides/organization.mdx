If you're bringing in colleagues, handing projects over to clients, or running several business lines, create an organization.

## Terminology

The platform interface uses the term **Organization** (`/organization`, the Create organization button in the context switcher menu). In everyday speech and marketing materials, you'll often see the synonym **Company** -- they mean the same thing.

In this documentation, we use "Organization" as the main term and mention "Company" the first time for those who are used to it.

---

## Personal account vs organization

The platform has two contexts at once, and you can switch between them in one click:

| Context | What's inside |
|---|---|
| **Personal account** | Personal balance, charge history (`/billing/words/history`), personal AI Employees, personal connections |
| **Organization** | Shared balance, shared agents, shared connections, separate member roles. Charge history is hidden in the organization context -- billing follows the organization's plan |

What you need to know:

- **Agents don't "move" automatically.** Personal agents stay in your personal context; organization agents stay inside the organization. Create an agent in the right context from the start.
- **Billing is separate.** Charges for a personal conversation come from your personal balance. Charges in an organization come from its own balance.
- **Only owners and admins can invite people to an organization.** Regular members see the list of others but can't add new ones.

## Context switcher

The context switcher button is at the top of the sidebar, above the main menu. Clicking it opens the "Select organization" drop-down menu.

The menu includes:

- **Current context** -- the checked item at the top (your personal account or one of your organizations).
- **Your organizations** -- with your role in each (Owner, Admin, Member).
- **Organization settings** -- goes to `/organization?tab=general` for the current organization.
- **Create organization** -- opens the form for creating a new one.

<Tip>
  Switch the context before you open a section -- the balance, chats, agents, and billing you see belong to the context you're currently in.
</Tip>

## Creating an organization

<Frame>
  <img src="/public/screenshots/05-org-create-form.png" alt="Organization creation form" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the context switcher">
    Click the context switcher button at the top of the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create organization">
    The button to create a new organization is at the bottom of the drop-down menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a name">
    The **Name** field is required. It's the organization's visible name in the switcher and in the header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a slug (optional)">
    The **Slug** field accepts Latin letters, digits, and hyphens. It's used in the URL (`/o/<slug>/...`). If you leave it empty, it's generated automatically from the name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create">
    After creation, the platform automatically switches the context to the new organization. You're its owner.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can create several organizations -- for example, for different clients or business lines. All of them are available in the switcher.
</Note>

## Organization settings

The `/organization` section has two tabs: **General** and **Members**.

### General tab

<Frame>
  <img src="/public/screenshots/07-org-settings.png" alt="Organization settings — General tab" />
</Frame>

Here you can:

- Upload the organization's **avatar**.
- Change the **name**.
- Change the **slug** (URL).

Changes are saved when you click the corresponding button. Be careful when changing the slug -- it changes public URLs.

### Members tab

<Frame>
  <img src="/public/screenshots/08-org-members.png" alt="Organization settings — Members tab" />
</Frame>

Here you'll find:

- A list of all organization members with a role badge.
- A button to invite a new member (available to owners and admins).
- Member actions -- changing a role or removing a member (available to owners and admins).

<Warning>
  If you're a member without admin rights, you'll see the alert "Failed to load invitations". This is expected: the invitation list is shown only to owners and administrators.
</Warning>

## Roles

An organization has three roles with different levels of access.

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Full control. Can do everything, including deleting the organization. Can't leave the organization (must transfer the role first). |
| **Admin** | Manages members (invitations, changing roles except the owner's, removal), billing, and organization settings. |
| **Member** | Regular access to agents, chats, and broadcasts. Doesn't see people management or billing tools. The Leave organization button is available. |

<Note>
  Each organization has exactly one owner. To transfer ownership, the current owner must promote another member to owner, after which they become an admin or can leave the organization.
</Note>

## Organization billing and balance

Each organization has a **separate balance** and a **separate plan**. Charges for the organization's agents come from its own balance, not your personal one.

What matters:

- In the organization context, the `/billing` page shows the organization's balance, available plans, and that organization's purchase history.
- **Charge history (`/billing/words/history`) is visible only in your personal account.** For organizations, this breakdown is hidden in the current version -- track usage with the summary metrics in [Billing](/en/guides/finance).
- The owner and admins can top up the organization's balance.

For details, see [Billing](/en/guides/finance).

## Inviting members

Available to owners and admins. The basic flow:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Members tab">
    `/organization?tab=members` in the context of the organization you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the invite button">
    Enter the invitee's email and choose a role (Admin or Member).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    The user receives a link by email. Once they accept, they appear in the member list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If an invitation is "stuck", ask the recipient to check their Spam folder and make sure the email address is correct.
</Tip>

---

## Removing members and leaving an organization

- **Leave organization** (Member or Admin role) -- a button on the members page or in settings. After you leave, you lose access to the organization's agents and data; they stay with the organization.
- **Remove member** (Owner, Admin) -- an action in the member list.
- **Delete the entire organization** -- Owner only, in settings. All of the organization's agents, chats, and profiles are permanently deleted.

<Warning>
  Deleting an organization can't be undone. If there's data you need to keep, export the chats and Knowledge Base first.
</Warning>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I have several organizations?">
    Yes, there's no limit. All organizations are available in the context switcher, and you can switch in one click.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I leave an organization?">
    You lose access to its agents, chats, and settings. The agents and data themselves stay with the organization -- they aren't deleted. If you come back via an invitation, your access is restored.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I invite a user to an organization?">
    Yes, but only owners and administrators can. Regular members don't see this option.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where can I see the organization's spending?">
    In the organization context, open the [Billing](/en/guides/finance) section -- it shows the balance and plans. The detailed word-by-word charge history is hidden in the organization context; use the summary metrics and the [Dashboard](/en/guides/dashboard).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is a personal account different from an organization?">
    A personal account is designed for a single user -- it has its own balance, agents, and history. An organization is for a team: a shared pool of agents, a shared balance, member roles, and joint management.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I have personal and organization agents at the same time?">
    Yes. The contexts are independent. Personal agents live in your personal account, organization agents in the organization. Use the switcher to move between them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I transfer ownership of an organization to another user?">
    The current owner promotes the chosen member to Owner on the Members tab. After that, the former owner becomes an administrator or can leave the organization.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use the same agent in my personal account and in an organization?">
    No -- an agent is tied to one context. If you need "the same" agent in an organization, copy the Persona and Knowledge Base into a new agent created inside the organization.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
