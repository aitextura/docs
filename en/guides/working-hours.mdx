## Section structure

The section has two sub-tabs:

| Sub-tab | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Work week** | The main schedule: time zone, working hours for each day of the week, quick presets |
| **Special days** | Exceptions to the main schedule: holidays, days off, days with a modified schedule |

<Note>
  The schedule applies to all channels connected to the AI Employee. If you need a different schedule for different channels, create separate AI Employees.
</Note>

---

## Work week

### Time zone

At the top of the section is the **Working time zone** selector. The AI Employee's entire schedule is tied to the selected time zone. The default is **Europe/Moscow (GMT+3)**.

<Warning>
  The time zone affects the entire schedule -- both the work week and special days. Make sure the right time zone is selected before you set up anything else. A mistake here means the AI Employee will work at hours other than the ones you expect.
</Warning>

### Daily schedule

Below the time zone selector is the schedule for each day of the week, Monday through Sunday. Each day has the following controls:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Start time** | The time the AI Employee starts replying (HH:MM format) |
| **End time** | The time after which the AI Employee stops replying (HH:MM format) |
| **Add interval** | Creates an additional working period within the same day |
| **Delete interval** | Removes the current time interval (trash icon) |
| **Disable day** | Makes the day a full day off (sun icon next to the day name) |

Each day can have **several intervals**. This is handy for breaks -- for example, two intervals, 09:00–13:00 and 14:00–18:00, create a lunch break from 13:00 to 14:00.

<Tip>
  If working hours are the same every day, set up Monday and then use the "Copy Mon to all days" preset.
</Tip>

### Quick settings (presets)

Below the schedule is the **Quick working hours settings** block -- a set of ready-made templates:

| Preset | What it sets |
|---|---|
| **24/7, no limits** | All days active, around the clock, no days off |
| **Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00** | A standard work week; Saturday and Sunday are days off |
| **Support: weekdays 10–19, Sat 10–16** | A support team schedule with a short Saturday |
| **Sat/Sun off** | Disables Saturday and Sunday; leaves other days unchanged |
| **Copy Mon to all days** | Applies Monday's schedule to all other days |

<Note>
  Presets are a starting point. After applying one, you can adjust any day manually.
</Note>

## Special days

The **Special days** sub-tab lets you set exceptions to the main schedule: public holidays, company days off, and days with a modified schedule.

**Special days always take priority** over the main work week schedule.

For each special day, specify:
- **Date** -- a specific calendar day
- **Parameters** -- a full day off or a modified schedule with different working hours

<Warning>
  You need to update special days every year. Holidays added for last year don't carry over to the next year automatically.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Add all public holidays in advance -- a year ahead. That way you can be sure the AI Employee works correctly on holidays.
</Tip>

## Behavior outside working hours

When a customer writes to the AI Employee outside working hours, there are two possible outcomes:

1. **The AI Employee doesn't reply** -- the message stays unanswered until the next working interval begins.
2. **The AI Employee sends an auto-reply** -- a preconfigured message with information about working hours.

<Warning>
  You set the auto-reply text not here but in the [Channel](/en/channels/index) section. The **Working Hours** tab only controls the schedule -- "when", not "what to do".
</Warning>

<Note>
  If you don't set up working hours, the AI Employee runs **24/7** by default -- all days are active from 00:00 to 23:59.
</Note>

## Step-by-step instructions

### Setting up a standard work week

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the section">
    Go to the AI Employee you need and open the **Working Hours** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a time zone">
    Make sure the correct time zone is selected in the **Working time zone** selector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set working hours">
    For each working day, set the **Start time** and **End time**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Disable days off">
    For days off, click the sun icon next to the day name to make them non-working.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. Changes take effect immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Or just click the **Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00** preset in the quick settings block -- the schedule fills in automatically.
</Tip>

### Adding a lunch break

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the day">
    In the work week schedule, find the day you want to add a break to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the first interval">
    Set the working time before the break -- for example, **09:00–13:00**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a second interval">
    Click **Add interval** and set the working time after the break -- for example, **14:00–18:00**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. Now the AI Employee won't reply to messages from 13:00 to 14:00.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Adding a special day (holiday)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the sub-tab">
    Open the **Special days** sub-tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an entry">
    Click the button to add a new special day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the parameters">
    Set the **date** and choose the type: a full day off or a modified schedule with different hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Turning on 24/7 mode

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the section">
    Go to the **Working Hours** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the preset">
    In the quick settings block, click **24/7, no limits**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. All days of the week become active from 00:00 to 23:59.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

<Accordion title="What happens when a customer writes outside working hours?">
  The AI Employee doesn't reply to messages or sends a preconfigured auto-reply. The exact behavior depends on the settings in the [Channel](/en/channels/index) section.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I set different schedules for different channels?">
  No. The schedule is set at the AI Employee level and applies to all of its channels at once. If you need a different schedule, create separate AI Employees.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What takes priority -- the main schedule or a special day?">
  Special days always take priority over the main work week schedule.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How quickly do changes take effect?">
  Immediately after saving. You don't need to wait or reload anything.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I set a break within a working day?">
  Yes. Use the **Add interval** button to split the working day into several periods. For example: 09:00–13:00 and 14:00–18:00.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do I need to update special days every year?">
  Yes. Holidays added for the current year don't carry over to the next one automatically. Review and update the list at the start of each year.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="If I don't set up working hours, will the AI Employee work 24/7?">
  Yes. By default, all days are active from 00:00 to 23:59, which means round-the-clock mode with no days off.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I set up an auto-reply for non-working hours?">
  You set the auto-reply text not here but in the [Channel](/en/channels/index) section. The **Working Hours** tab only controls the schedule.
</Accordion>
