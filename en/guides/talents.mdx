Talents are an AI Employee's hands. Without them, it can only talk; connect a talent and it starts taking action in your service: checking available dates in your PMS, creating a deal in your CRM, reading and editing Google Sheets, creating a calendar event.

## Two subtabs

**App overview** is the catalog of everything you can connect. **Connected** shows what's already working for the employee you're configuring. Talents are connected to each employee separately.

## What you can connect

More than 3,000 services: CRM and ERP, hotel PMS, stores and payments, messengers, email and mailings, spreadsheets and documents, databases, AI services. Search by name or narrow the list with a category filter.

## How to connect

Click a talent's card and follow the instructions in the window — usually you paste an API key or sign in to the service with your account. Then click **Connect talent**, and it appears on the **Connected** subtab.

<Note>Service-specific walkthroughs — Bitrix24, amoCRM, Travelline, Bnovo, MEWS, and others — are in the [detailed guide to connecting talents](/en/guides/connect-talents).</Note>

## Describe it in the Persona

A connected talent gives the employee an ability, but not a reason to use it. Always spell out in the [Persona](/en/guides/persona) when and under what conditions it should use the talent. Example rules for each service are in the [detailed guide to connecting talents](/en/guides/connect-talents).

## Your own talent

If the service you need isn't in the catalog, connect your own MCP server with the **Create talent** button. This is a job for a developer: the server has to be written and hosted. If someone on your team codes — even with an AI assistant — they can handle it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fill in the basics">
    The talent's **Name** and **Description** — what your server can do. The employee sees the name in its list of tools.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Specify the server">
    **Server type** — **SSE** or **Streamable HTTP**, depending on the implementation. **Server URL** — an address like `https://example.com/mcp`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose authorization">
    **No authorization**, **Bearer Token**, **API Key**, or **Basic Auth**. Add your own **HTTP headers** if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Test connection">
    The platform contacts the server and shows whether it responds. The **Tools** tab lists the functions the server exposes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm trust and create">
    Check **I trust this server** and click **Create talent**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The talent is connected, but the employee doesn't use it">
    The [Persona](/en/guides/persona) has no rule for when to call it. Add a line like "when the customer asks about [situation], use [tool name]" and check it in the test chat. Example rules for each service are in the [detailed guide to connecting talents](/en/guides/connect-talents).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is a talent different from a channel?">
    A channel is where messages come from (Telegram, WhatsApp, Bitrix24 Open Channels). A talent is what the employee can do in response.

    Bitrix24 and amoCRM exist in both places: the channel is for talking to the customer, the talent is for working inside the CRM.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The employee stopped performing actions it used to perform">
    Most likely the authorization broke: the token expired, the webhook was revoked, or the service password changed. Open the **Connected** subtab and reconnect the talent.

    If that doesn't help, contact [Support](/en/guides/support).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I connect the same talent twice?">
    Not to the same employee: a talent is connected to it once. But you can connect the same talent to different employees, each with its own settings.

    Always give connected talents clear names — otherwise you won't be able to tell them apart.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many talents can I connect to one employee?">
    There's no limit. But each talent has to be described in the [Persona](/en/guides/persona) — when exactly the employee uses it. The more talents you add, the more tangled this logic gets and the more often the employee picks at random, so don't overdo it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if the service is unavailable?">
    The employee can't perform the action or get data from your service. It keeps replying, but only based on the Persona and the Knowledge Base.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are talents included in the plan?">
    Talents themselves don't cost extra. A third-party service may charge on its side — a subscription or payment fees.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
