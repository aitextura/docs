title: "Chats"
description: "All of your AI Employees' conversations with customers in one list: find a conversation, reply to the customer manually, and understand why the employee answered the way it did."
---

A customer wrote at night, the employee replied — and in the morning you want to know what happened and step in if needed. **Chats** gathers the conversations of all your AI Employees across all channels into one list: read them, reply to customers yourself, and review every answer the employee gave.

## Find a conversation

The left column is a shared list of conversations across all employees and channels. The most recent conversations are at the top.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/images/en/screenshots/chats_1.png" alt="The Chats section: search, All employees and All channels filters, conversation list on the left" />
</Frame>

The **Search chat...** field searches by customer name everywhere at once: type "Egorchenko" and you'll get their conversations in Telegram bots for different projects and in email, in a single list. Next to it are two filters: **All employees** — keep only one AI Employee's conversations, and **All channels** — keep only one connection.

<Tip>Filters combine. Select an employee and the Telegram channel, and you'll see only that employee's Telegram conversations. This is a handy way to check answer quality in one channel.</Tip>

What a card in the list contains:

| Element | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The customer's name, or, if it couldn't be determined, the channel or company name |
| **Red circle with a number** | How many messages you haven't read yet |
| **"employee · channel" line** | Who's handling the conversation and which connection it came through: "Anna · @GreenParkTestBOT" |
| **Date and time** | When the last message arrived |
| **Pink "AI off" badge** | The employee is stopped in this chat, and you're expected to reply |

## Conversation window

Click a conversation to open it on the right. The header shows the customer's name, the caption "with [employee name] · [channel]," and the **•••** button — the chat control menu.

<Frame>
  <img src="/public/screenshots/87-chat-menu-options.png" alt="An open conversation: the red AI stopped banner, a customer message on the left, a manager message, a pause label, an employee reply, and the input field" />
</Frame>

Customer messages appear on the left in gray; the AI Employee's replies appear on the right in blue, labeled with its name. Days are separated by a date chip, and the platform marks system events with a label in the center of the feed: "Chat paused after a manager's message," "Conversation history cleared."

The red **AI stopped** banner appears when the employee is turned off in this specific chat. Next to it are two buttons: **Turn on AI** brings the employee back into the conversation, and **Clear history** erases the context it keeps in mind.

<Note>**Clear history** doesn't delete the conversation from **Chats** — it only resets what the employee takes into account for its next reply.</Note>

## Reply yourself

The **Enter a message...** field at the bottom is for your manual reply to the customer. The message goes to the same channel the conversation is in: if the customer wrote in Telegram, they get it in Telegram — no need to switch anywhere.

As soon as you send a message, the AI Employee pauses in this conversation so it doesn't interrupt a live person.

| What happens | How it works |
|---|---|
| Customer messages | Keep arriving and being saved in the conversation |
| AI Employee | Stays silent while you handle the conversation |
| Return | After 10–20 minutes without activity from you, the employee returns to the conversation on its own |

You can bring it back sooner with the **Turn on AI** button above the feed or the **Resume AI** item in the chat menu. If the employee hasn't returned even after half an hour, the **Stop after manager reply** setting is enabled on the [Additional Settings](/en/guides/additional-settings) tab — that's also where you configure text commands a manager can use to stop and start the employee right from the messenger.

## Response analysis

The chart icon under an AI Employee's reply opens the **Response analysis** panel on the right — a full trace of how that reply was put together. This is the first thing to open when the employee answered wrong; otherwise you're editing the [Persona](/en/guides/persona) blindly.

| Panel block | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Agent response** | The text sent to the customer |
| **Why the agent answered this way** | Whether something similar was found in [Self-Learning](/en/guides/self-learning), whether anything was found in the [Knowledge Base](/en/guides/knowledge), or whether the employee answered from the model's general knowledge |
| **Language detection** | Which language the platform detected in the customer's message |
| **What the agent did** | Which [talents](/en/guides/talents) it called and how that went |
| **Final decision** | One line: what the customer ultimately got |
| **Message log** | Step-by-step processing with timestamps — security check, assembling the final reply |
| **Technical data** | Raw JSON for developers |

At the bottom of the panel is the **Get recommendations** button: the platform analyzes this specific case and suggests targeted changes to the employee's settings.

<Tip>Often there's nothing to fix: the employee simply didn't find a document in the Knowledge Base or didn't call the right talent. The analysis shows this right away, before you rewrite the instruction.</Tip>

## Reporting a bad answer

The thumbs-down under an employee's reply opens the **What went wrong?** panel. It's not a rating — it's a fork into two scenarios.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the problem">
    In your own words: "replied in the wrong language," "made up delivery terms," "ignored the question about price."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the closest option">
    **Made up facts**, **Didn't understand the question**, **Didn't use knowledge**, **Tone / rudeness**, **Response length**, or **Other**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Fix with AI">
    The platform analyzes the case in the background — you can keep working — and shows the proposed settings changes before applying them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the instruction isn't the issue, click **The problem isn't the instruction — it's a technical error**: this opens the **Contact support** form with the fields "What went wrong" and "What the reply should have contained." The case report and a link to the message are attached to the request automatically — no need to retell the conversation. What happens to the request next is covered in [Support](/en/guides/support).

## Chat menu

The **•••** button in the conversation header opens a menu with two blocks: controls for this chat and navigation.

<Frame>
  <img src="/public/screenshots/86-chat-actions-menu.png" alt="The ••• menu: chat controls — share link, resume AI, hide deleted messages, two reports, erase memory; navigation — customer profile, open employee" />
</Frame>

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Share link** | Gives a direct link to view the conversation that opens without signing in to the platform |
| **Stop AI** / **Resume AI** | Turns the employee off and on in this chat only, without affecting other conversations |
| **Show deleted messages** | Brings messages previously deleted from the chat back into the feed. They're hidden by default |
| **Report (all messages)** | A line-by-line export of the conversation with roles, timestamps, and a log under each employee reply |
| **Report (without archive)** | The same, but without the archived part of the history |
| **Erase memory** | Resets the context the employee has accumulated for this customer |
| **Customer profile** | Opens the customer card — [Profiles](/en/guides/profiles) |
| **Open employee** | Goes to the settings of the AI Employee handling this chat |

<Warning>**Erase memory** can't be undone: the employee will forget everything you agreed on with the customer in this conversation. The label "Conversation history cleared" remains in the feed.</Warning>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where did the employee get this answer?">
    Click the chart icon under the message — **Response analysis** shows what was found in the Knowledge Base and Self-Learning, which talents fired, and why the reply turned out the way it did. Start your review of any mistake here.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cleared the history, but the employee still remembers the name and details">
    The name, phone number, and preferences are stored separately — in the [customer profile](/en/guides/profiles), and clearing the conversation doesn't affect them. To make the employee forget those too, clear the profile.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The employee starts greeting the customer again mid-conversation">
    Automatic history clearing kicked in: the channel settings have too short an interval, so the context is reset during the conversation. Increase the interval or turn off automatic clearing in the [channel settings](/en/channels/index).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I turn off the employee in one chat without affecting the others?">
    **•••** menu → **Stop AI**. A red banner appears, and you'll be the one replying in this conversation. To silence the employee across all channels at once, turn the employee itself off in the [AI Employees](/en/guides/ai-employees) list.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can the customer tell that a human is replying instead of the AI?">
    No. Your message comes from the same bot or account the customer was talking to the employee through — the switch is invisible to them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if two managers reply in the same chat at the same time?">
    The customer gets both messages — the platform doesn't merge them or block anyone. Agree within your team on who's handling each conversation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The employee stopped replying after a certain number of messages">
    The **Message limit** kicked in — a cap on the number of replies in one chat per day. It's set on the [Additional Settings](/en/guides/additional-settings) tab; if the field is empty, there's no limit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I know the employee handed the conversation over to me?">
    Through a Telegram notification. The handover itself isn't configured here, but with triggers in [Notifications](/en/guides/notifications): until a trigger exists, the conversation won't pause and you won't get anything, even if the employee promised the customer to call a manager.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Fine points

You don't need these for everyday work. Come back here when you need to dig into a tricky case or show a conversation to a colleague.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Share a conversation with someone who has no platform access">
    **•••** menu → **Share link**. The **Share chat** window opens with a link like `app.aitextura.com/live/…` — it opens the conversation in a browser without signing in to the platform, and you can reply to the customer under your own name from the same page.

    <Warning>Anyone who gets the link can see the conversation. Don't send it if the conversation contains the customer's personal data.</Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Chat report">
    **Report (all messages)** lays out the conversation line by line: role (`user` or `assistant`), exact time, message ID, and an expanded log under each employee reply. At the top are **Copy all** and **Expand all**. The format is designed to be handed over in full to Support or a developer.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Link to a specific message">
    Under an employee's reply there's a short code like `01351f` — clicking it copies it. This makes it easy to reference a single message when talking to colleagues or in a support request, without retelling the whole conversation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What you can do with an individual message">
    The action bar appears only under AI Employee replies — customer messages can only be deleted.

    | Icon | What it does |
    |---|---|
    | Copy | Copies the reply text to the clipboard |
    | Thumbs down | Opens **What went wrong?** |
    | Chart | Opens **Response analysis** |
    | Message code | Copies the short ID |
    | Trash | Deletes the message from the chat and from the employee's context |
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The customer profile fills itself in">
    The **Customer profile** item opens a card with the customer's data: name, phone, needs, and other fields from connected templates. You don't need to enter anything manually — the AI Employee fills them in as the conversation goes. A new customer's card is empty, and that's normal. See [Profiles](/en/guides/profiles) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
