You don't need to study anything up front to get your AI Employee answering customers: the platform walks you through five steps and suggests the next action at each one. Below is the same path in words, from creating an agent to the first conversation in Telegram.

## Create an agent

Open **AI Agents** and click **Hire Agent**. The platform asks how you want to build it.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/quickstart_how.png" alt="The “How should we create the agent?” screen with three options: automatically from a website, from a template, from scratch" />
</Frame>

| Option | When to choose it |
|---|---|
| **Automatically from a website** | You have a website that describes your services: the platform reads it and builds the agent for you |
| **From a template** | A ready-made role for a typical task — the fastest way to start |
| **From scratch** | A builder: you write the instructions yourself, step by step |

Let's take a template. The catalog has many roles — sales, tech support, recruiting, real estate, insurance, personal assistant, and more.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/quickstart_templates.png" alt="The “Choose a template” screen with role cards: Sales Agent, Tech Support, HR Recruiter, Personal Assistant, Real Estate Agent, Insurance Agent" />
</Frame>

Once you pick one, the agent is created and appears in your list.

## Connect Telegram

Right after creation, the platform offers your first channel — Telegram. You don't need to enter a token.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/quickstart_telegram.png" alt="The “Connect Telegram” screen with a “Create Telegram bot” button and an “I already have a bot — enter token” link" />
</Frame>

Click **Create Telegram bot** — Telegram opens with a bot creation window ready to go. Enter a name and a username (it must end in `bot`) and click **Create**. You set the bot's picture and description there too, in Telegram.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/quickstart_telegram_bot.png" alt="The bot creation window in Telegram: “Bot name” and “Username” fields and a “Create” button" />
</Frame>

This is the bot your customers will use to message your AI Employee. If you already have a bot, choose **I already have a bot — enter token**.

Telegram is just the first channel. You can connect WhatsApp, Avito, VK, website chat, CRMs, and Ticket Systems later in [Channels](/en/channels) — your AI Employee answers in all of them at once.

## Upload knowledge

Next, the platform asks you to add sources — the material your AI Employee will answer from: price lists, policies, service descriptions.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/quickstart_knowledge.png" alt="The “Knowledge Base” screen with four ways to add a source: files, text, YouTube video, web page" />
</Frame>

| Option | What fits |
|---|---|
| **Upload files** | PDF, DOCX, TXT, CSV, and other documents |
| **Text** | A short rule, an address, contacts — paste it right here |
| **YouTube video** | A video link; the platform extracts the text from it |
| **Web page** | A website, Google Docs, or Sheets — any URL |

To learn how your AI Employee searches these materials, see [Knowledge Base](/en/guides/knowledge).

## Add Quick Answers

Here you set question–answer pairs for the things customers ask most often. When such a question comes in, your AI Employee replies with your text word for word, without rephrasing.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/quickstart_quick_answers.png" alt="The “Quick Answers” screen with “Question” and “Answer” fields and an “Add answer” button" />
</Frame>

## Set working hours

The last step: when your AI Employee answers customers and when it stays silent.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/quickstart_hours.png" alt="The “Working hours” screen: workday selection, “from” and “to” times, time zone" />
</Frame>

Select the workdays, set the time range, and check the time zone — by default, your AI Employee works around the clock. You can set up breaks, holidays, and an out-of-hours auto-reply later in [Working Hours](/en/guides/working-hours).

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/quickstart_ready.png" alt="The “Agent is ready” screen with a settings summary and a “Go to agent” button" />
</Frame>

## Refine the instructions

The **Go to agent** button opens the editor — this is where all of your AI Employee's settings live, along with a test chat where you can talk to it.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/images/ru/screenshots/quickstart_editor.png" alt="The agent editor, Persona tab: technical name, display name, an “Improve instructions” button, and instruction blocks" />
</Frame>

From here, work in a loop: message your AI Employee in the test chat as a customer, and if you don't like the reply, click **Improve instructions** and describe in words what to change. The AI Employee rewrites the relevant blocks itself, and you check the result with your next message. Repeat until you're happy with the replies.

<Tip>After a few edits, click **Clear history** in the test chat: old replies and conversation memory throw the AI Employee off, and you can't tell whether an edit helped.</Tip>

That's it — your AI Employee is ready: it answers in Telegram, using your materials, during your hours.
