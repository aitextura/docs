Auto Follow-up sends automatic follow-up messages. If a customer stops replying, the AI Employee writes to them after a set delay -- gently, in the context of the conversation, with no copy-paste.

<Frame><img src="/public/screenshots/36-agent-followup.png" alt="Auto Follow-up — trigger and reminder chain settings on the agent tab" /></Frame>

<Warning>
  **Auto Follow-up lives in two different places in the interface.**

  - **The Auto Follow-up section** (`/followup`) in the platform's left menu -- a monitoring panel for all chats with scheduled reminders across all AI Employees.
  - **The Auto Follow-up tab** in a specific AI Employee's settings -- this is where you turn the feature on, set delays, and write instructions for each step.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Auto Follow-up is also available **globally** as a separate `/followup` section in the platform sidebar -- a summary monitoring panel across all AI Employees at once (see "Monitoring panel" below). You set up chains on a specific AI Employee's tab.
</Note>

## Monitoring panel (left menu)

The **Auto Follow-up** section in the left menu is a single read-only panel that shows all chats with scheduled follow-up messages across all your AI Employees. You don't set up chains here -- you watch what's already running.

### Search

The search bar at the top of the page reads **"Search by name, employee, or customer"**. You can search by:

- customer name (for example, "Michael")
- AI Employee name (for example, "Alex")
- any keyword from the chat name

### Filter by employee

Below the search bar is the **All employees** drop-down. By default, it shows chats for all AI Employees. Select a specific AI Employee to see only their chats.

### Chats table

The main part of the page is a table with two columns:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Chat** | The customer's name or the name of the chat where Auto Follow-up is active |
| **Employee** | The name of the AI Employee handling this conversation who will send the follow-up |

Each row is one active chat where the customer went silent and an automatic reminder is scheduled. Click a row to open the full conversation history.

<Tip>
  If one AI Employee constantly has lots of chats in follow-up, consider reviewing their [Persona](/en/guides/persona) or improving the first reply.
</Tip>

<Note>
  This section shows only chats where Auto Follow-up is active. If an AI Employee has Auto Follow-up turned off, their chats won't appear here.
</Note>

## Setting up Auto Follow-up (AI Employee tab)

Open the AI Employee you need and go to the **Auto Follow-up** tab.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/images/en/screenshots/auto_followup_1.png" />
</Frame>

### Turning it on and off

At the top of the screen is the **Enable Auto Follow-up** toggle -- it turns the feature on or off entirely for this AI Employee.

### Chain of steps

Below the toggle are the **steps** -- consecutive reminders the AI Employee sends to the customer one after another if they stay silent.

#### Step parameters

| Parameter | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Delay (H / MIN)** | Number fields | How long to wait before sending the reminder (hours and minutes) |
| **Message instruction** | Text field (required) | A description of what the AI Employee should write to the customer. The AI Employee writes the text on its own based on the instruction and the conversation context |
| **Run once per chat** | Checkbox | If checked, the step fires only once for the whole chat -- even if the customer goes silent again |

### How the chain works

<Steps>
  <Step title="The customer goes silent">
    The first step's timer starts after the customer's last message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="First reminder">
    When the first step's delay runs out, the AI Employee sends a reminder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Next steps">
    If the customer stays silent, each next step's delay counts from when the previous reminder was sent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The customer replied -- reset">
    If the customer sends a message at any point, the timer resets and the chain starts over from the first step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Step-by-step setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Auto Follow-up tab">
    Go to the settings of the AI Employee you need and select the **Auto Follow-up** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the toggle">
    Switch **Enable Auto Follow-up** on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a step">
    Click **Add step**, set the delay (hours/minutes), and write the message instruction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the chain">
    Add several steps with increasing delays for a complete reminder chain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click the save button. From then on, when a customer goes silent, the chat automatically appears in the **Auto Follow-up** section in the left menu.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Example: a three-step chain

| Step | Delay | Instruction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 hours | Remind the user that we're here and ready to help |
| 2 | 24 hours | Ask whether the question is still relevant |
| 3 | 48 hours | Say a warm goodbye and let them know we're always happy to help if needed |

## Recommendations

<Warning>
  Don't overdo frequent reminders. The recommended pause between steps is **at least 2-4 hours**. Messages that are too frequent annoy customers and can lead to blocking.
</Warning>

<Accordion title="How to write good step instructions">
  An instruction isn't the finished message text -- it's a task for the AI. The AI Employee reads the instruction, looks at the conversation context, and writes the message on its own.

  **Good:**
  > "Gently remind them we're here. Ask if they have any questions left. Don't be pushy."

  **Bad:**
  > "Write: Hello! We'd like to remind you that you can..."

  **More examples of good instructions:**
  - "Check whether the customer has made a decision. Offer help if they need more information."
  - "Remind them of the special offer we discussed. Don't pressure them, just refresh their memory."
  - "Ask if everything is okay. If the customer doesn't reply, say a warm goodbye."

  The more specific the task, the better the result. But don't write the finished text -- give the AI Employee room to phrase the message naturally.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How it works with other settings">
  **[Persona](/en/guides/persona).** The reminder style depends on the AI Employee's Persona. If the Persona sets a friendly, informal tone, reminders will follow that style too. Auto Follow-up doesn't overwrite the Persona -- it works on top of it.

  **[Working Hours](/en/guides/working-hours).** If the AI Employee has a schedule, reminders are sent only during working hours. Make sure the delays and the schedule don't conflict.

  **[Channels](/en/channels/index).** Auto Follow-up works in all of the AI Employee's connected channels -- Telegram, WhatsApp, the website widget, and others.
</Accordion>

## FAQ

<Accordion title="Why is the Auto Follow-up section empty even though there are active chats?">
  Most likely, Auto Follow-up isn't turned on for any AI Employee. Check the **Auto Follow-up** tab in each AI Employee's settings -- the toggle must be on and at least one step with an instruction must be added.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if the customer replies before a step fires?">
  The timer resets. The countdown starts over from the first step.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does the AI Employee send the exact text from the instruction?">
  No. You write an instruction -- a description of the task -- and the AI Employee writes the message on its own, taking into account the conversation context and its Persona.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How many steps can I add?">
  There's no limit on the number of steps. In practice, 2-3 steps work best. More than three reminders usually feel like spam.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is the delay counted from the customer's last message?">
  Only for the first step. Each next step's delay counts from when the previous reminder was sent.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Auto Follow-up work in all channels?">
  Yes, in all of the AI Employee's connected channels.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Will Auto Follow-up send a message outside working hours?">
  If the AI Employee has working hours set up, the reminder waits until working hours begin.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Auto Follow-up use tokens?">
  Yes, each reminder sent uses tokens just like a regular AI Employee reply.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I turn off Auto Follow-up for a single chat?">
  No, Auto Follow-up is configured at the AI Employee level and works for all of its chats. If a customer asks you not to write to them, add a matching instruction to the Persona.
</Accordion>
