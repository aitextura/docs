The **Rules Hub** is a central store of instruction blocks you can use when configuring the [Persona](/en/guides/persona) of any AI Employee. Instead of writing instructions from scratch every time, you take a ready-made block from the hub, insert it into the [Persona](/en/guides/persona), and adapt it to your needs.

The Rules Hub is a **global library of reusable Role / Tone / Instruction blocks**, available across all your organizations and for all AI Employees. One block, many employees: set up a universal tone or scenario once and apply it to any agent.

The Rules Hub is in the platform's left menu. Clicking it expands a submenu with two sections: **Presets** and **My rules**.

## Rules Hub structure

The hub has two sections. Each is available both from the left menu and through the **Apply template** button inside the [Persona](/en/guides/persona) editor.

| Section | What's inside | Who creates it |
|---|---|---|
| **Presets** | Ready-made block templates for popular roles and scenarios | The AI Textura team |
| **My rules** | Blocks you saved yourself from an employee's [Persona](/en/guides/persona) | You |

Both sections work the same way: you find the block you need, click it, and it's added to the selected employee's [Persona](/en/guides/persona). You can then edit the block for your specific task.

## Presets — templates from the AI Textura team

**Presets** are a library of professionally written blocks covering typical roles and scenarios. The AI Textura team built them from real cases so you can launch an employee quickly without lengthy setup.

<Frame><img src="/public/screenshots/55-rulehub-presets.png" alt="Presets — AI TEXTURA template catalog" /></Frame>

### Full list of AI TEXTURA presets

Each preset belongs to one of the three [Persona](/en/guides/persona) block types. Below is the complete current catalog:

#### Role — who your employee is

| Preset | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Hotel administrator** | Hotel chains, apartments, small hotels — taking bookings and working with guests |
| **Realtor** | Real estate agencies — lead qualification, property showings, deal support |
| **Personal assistant** | A general-purpose helper — schedules, tasks, simple errands |
| **Consultant** | Sales and customer service — expert advice on a product or service |

#### Tone — how the employee talks

| Preset | When to use |
|---|---|
| **Neutral** | The default professional tone — fits most businesses |
| **Humorous** | Lifestyle projects, B2C products with a young audience — the employee jokes and keeps the style lively |
| **Empathetic** | Healthcare, education, psychological support — attentive to the customer's feelings |

#### Instruction — step-by-step scenarios

| Preset | What it describes |
|---|---|
| **Booking (hotel)** | A booking scenario: dates, guests, payment, confirmation |
| **Real estate sales** | Customer qualification: budget, area, purpose of purchase, handover to a manager |

<Tip>
  If your role isn't in the catalog, take the closest one and adapt the text. For example, "Consultant" turns into "Auto loan consultant" by adding 5–10 lines of specifics. That's faster than writing everything from scratch.
</Tip>

### Searching templates

The **Presets** section offers two tools:

- **Search by name** — enter a keyword, such as "hotel" or "booking"
- **"All types" filter** — pick a specific block type (Role, Tone, Script, etc.) to narrow the list

### How to apply a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Option 1 — from the Rules Hub (left menu)">
    1. Open **Rules Hub** → **Presets** in the left menu
    2. Find the template you need using search or the filter
    3. Click the template
    4. Choose the employee you want to apply it to
    5. The block is added to the employee's [Persona](/en/guides/persona)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Option 2 — from the Persona editor (quick access)">
    1. Open the employee → the [Persona](/en/guides/persona) tab
    2. Click **Apply template**
    3. In the window that opens, select the **Presets** tab
    4. Find the template and click it — the blocks are added to the [Persona](/en/guides/persona)
    5. Edit the text to fit your specifics
    6. Save your changes
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Templates are a starting point, not a finished solution. Always adapt the text to your business: replace names, add concrete details, remove what you don't need. The more precisely an instruction describes your situation, the more predictably the employee behaves.
</Tip>

## My rules — your personal library

**My rules** is where the blocks you saved from any employee's [Persona](/en/guides/persona) are stored. It's your personal library of reusable instructions.

Typical use cases:

- The same rules for several employees (for example, "don't make up information" or "don't ask more than two questions in one message")
- A universal communication tone applied to all of the company's employees
- Proven scenarios you've polished on one employee and want to reuse

### How to save a block to My rules

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the employee's Persona">
    Go to the employee and open the [Persona](/en/guides/persona) tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the block">
    Find the block you want to save and click the **"..."** icon (block menu) → **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The block is available in the Hub">
    The block appears in **My rules** — both in the left menu and in the **Apply template** window inside the [Persona](/en/guides/persona) editor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How to apply a saved block to another employee

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the target employee's Persona">
    Go to the employee → the [Persona](/en/guides/persona) tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open templates">
    Click **Apply template** → go to the **My rules** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply and adapt">
    Click the block you need — it's added to the [Persona](/en/guides/persona). Edit the text if needed and save your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Rules in My rules are grouped by type: Role, Tone, Script, custom. Use the "All types" filter and name search to find blocks quickly.
</Note>

### Creating a rule directly in the Hub

Besides saving blocks from a [Persona](/en/guides/persona), you can create a new rule right in the Hub, without tying it to a specific employee:

1. Open **Rules Hub** → **My rules** in the left menu
2. Click **Add rule**
3. Set the name and block type
4. Write the instruction text
5. Save

The new rule appears in your library immediately and can be applied to any employee.

## The "/" command — quick import from the editor

In the [Persona](/en/guides/persona) editor, you can insert blocks from the Hub with the **/** command, invoked at the end of the editor:

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| **/** → **Import from hub** → **My rules** | Inserts one of your saved blocks |
| **/** → **Import from hub** → **Public** | Inserts a template from the AI Textura team |

## Ways into the Rules Hub

| From | Where you land | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| Left menu → **Rules Hub** → **Presets** | Hub page, "Presets" tab | All public templates from the AI Textura team |
| Left menu → **Rules Hub** → **My rules** | Hub page, "My rules" tab | Your saved blocks |
| [Persona](/en/guides/persona) editor → **Apply template** | A pop-up window with two tabs | The same data: Presets and My rules |
| [Persona](/en/guides/persona) editor → **/** → **Import from hub** | Source selection: My rules / Public | Quickly insert a block into the current [Persona](/en/guides/persona) |

<Warning>
  Blocks from the Rules Hub are inserted into a [Persona](/en/guides/persona) **as a copy**. If you later change a block in the Hub, copies already inserted into employees' Personas are not affected. Each copy has to be updated separately.
</Warning>

## FAQ

<Accordion title="What's the difference between Presets and My rules?">
  Presets are ready-made templates from the AI Textura team, available to all users. My rules are your own blocks that you saved or created. Presets can't be edited in the Hub — only after you apply them to an employee. You have full control over My rules.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="If I change a block in My rules, will it update for all employees?">
  No. When you apply a block from the Hub to an employee, a copy is inserted into the [Persona](/en/guides/persona). Changes to the original don't affect copies already inserted, and vice versa. To update a block for several employees, you have to do it manually for each one.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use one template for several employees?">
  Yes. Apply the template to one employee, then to another — each gets its own copy that you can edit independently.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a limit on the number of saved rules?">
  No. You can save as many blocks as you need. We recommend giving blocks clear names and using filters so you don't get lost.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I delete a block from My rules?">
  Yes. Deleting a block from the Hub doesn't affect employees who already have it applied — they keep their own copy.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does the order of applied templates affect the employee's behavior?">
  Yes. Blocks placed higher in the [Persona](/en/guides/persona) have higher priority. After applying a template, you can drag blocks into the order you want right in the editor.
</Accordion>
