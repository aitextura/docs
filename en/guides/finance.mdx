## Page overview

When you open the section, you see several blocks from top to bottom:

| # | Block | Purpose |
|---|-------|---------|
| 1 | **Word Balance** | Progress bar and usage numbers |
| 2 | **Bonus Mode** | Toggle between bonus / paid words |
| 3 | **Summary Cards** | Key totals: messages, chats, words |
| 4 | **Filters** | Period and employee selection |
| 5 | **Analytics** | Four charts |
| 6 | **Billing History Table** | Daily breakdown |

---

## Word Balance

At the top of the page you'll find the balance block:

| Element | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Words used** | How many words have been spent out of the total limit (e.g., 1,475,624 of 14,200,000) |
| **Progress bar** | Visual bar with usage percentage (e.g., "90% used") |
| **Top Up Balance button** | Navigate to the top-up page |

<Warning>
  When usage approaches 90-95%, it is time to top up your balance. If words run out, all AI employees pause and clients stop receiving responses.
</Warning>

The balance is also displayed in two places on every page:
- In the **bottom left corner** of the sidebar — dollar amount and remaining words
- In the **top right corner** — button showing the current balance in dollars

---

## Bonus Mode

Below the balance block there is a toggle with two tabs:

| Mode | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Excluding Bonuses** | Actual expenses — only what was deducted from your paid balance |
| **Including Bonuses** | Total usage, including words received through bonus programs and promotions |

<Tip>
  The default mode is **Excluding Bonuses**. If you participate in the [referral program](/en/guides/referral) or have received bonus words, switch to the second mode to see the full picture.
</Tip>

## Summary Cards

Three cards with key figures for the selected period:

| Metric | What it means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Messages** | Total number of messages and their total cost | 5,308 ($0.0000) |
| **Chats** | Number of unique conversations and their total cost | 424 ($0.000) |
| **Words** | Total number of words used | 12,724,376 |

The header above the cards shows the period — by default **"All time"**. When filters are applied, the numbers recalculate. The **Messages** card is clickable — press **Details** for a breakdown.

---

## Filters

The **Filters** button lets you narrow down data by two parameters:

- **By period** — a specific date range (week, month, or custom interval)
- **By employee** — expenses for a specific AI employee

<Tip>
  The employee filter is especially useful when you have multiple AI employees. You'll quickly identify which one consumes the most resources, so you can optimize its instructions or [knowledge base](/en/guides/knowledge).
</Tip>

After applying filters, all data on the page — summary cards, charts, and table — recalculates accordingly.

## Analytics — four charts

The **Analytics** section contains four charts available at `/billing/words/history`. The block can be collapsed and expanded.

| # | Chart | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Words and messages by day** | How many words and messages were used each day in the period |
| 2 | **Cost by day** | Daily spending in dollars + maximum and average daily cost |
| 3 | **Chats and messages by day** | Client activity: number of new dialogs and messages |
| 4 | **Monthly cost trend** | Long-term dynamics — total spending and average daily cost by month |

### 1. Words and messages by day

A combined chart with two axes:
- **Left axis** — number of words (thousands)
- **Right axis** — number of messages

Shows the relationship between words and messages: some days have few messages but high word usage (long dialogs), and vice versa.

### 2. Cost by day

A line chart of daily expenses in dollars. Above the chart:
- **Maximum** daily cost (e.g., $48.89)
- **Average** daily cost (e.g., $4.42)

Helps identify peak days and understand their cause.

### 3. Chats and messages by day

A combined chart:
- **Left axis** — number of chats (dialogs)
- **Right axis** — number of messages

Shows client activity: how many new dialogs were opened and how many messages were processed.

### 4. Monthly cost trend

A long-term chart with two lines:
- **Total expenses ($)** — total spending per month
- **Average daily expenses ($)** — averaged daily cost

Shows the overall trend for budget planning.

## Billing History table

A detailed table broken down by day. Each row represents one day with charges.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Date** | Day of the charge (DD.MM.YYYY) |
| **Type** | Charge type (if applicable) |
| **Words** | Number of words used that day |
| **Messages** | Number of messages that day |
| **Cost** | Total cost for the day in dollars |
| **Dialogs** | Number of unique chats that day |
| **Per dialog** | Average cost per dialog |
| **Per message** | Average cost per message |

<Tip>
  Table rows are clickable — click any row to see a detailed breakdown for that day.
</Tip>

Pagination is available at the bottom of the table for navigating through pages.

## Pricing plans

AI Textura uses a word-based pricing model:

**Cost = Number of words x Price per word**

The price per word depends on your plan. The larger the word package, the lower the cost per word.

### Current plans

| Plan | Price | Word volume | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | $100 | 2,000,000 words | Launch, testing, 1–2 AI employees |
| **Pro** | $500 | 10,000,000 words | Active use, several AI employees, mid-volume client traffic |
| **Business** | $1000 | 20,000,000 words | Chains, agencies, high client traffic, or large [knowledge bases](/en/guides/knowledge) |

<Frame><img src="/public/screenshots/63-billing-topup.png" alt="Top-up page with plan options" /></Frame>

<Tip>
  The price per word on the **Business** plan is lower than on **Starter** because of the package size. If you steadily spend more than 2M words a month, switching to a larger package is more economical. Track current consumption in the [Billing History table](#billing-history-table) and on the charts.
</Tip>

### Regions and payment methods

The platform supports two regional payment gateways:

| Region | Payment methods |
|---|---|
| **Russia** | Bank transfer (to a settlement account), invoice payment (the platform issues an invoice), cryptocurrency |
| **World** | International cards and payment systems |

Conversion rate for ruble payments: **$1 = 100 ₽**.

<Note>
  The **Cryptocurrency** method is available in the "Russia" region — convenient if you don't have Russian payment details or if your card doesn't pass through international gateways.
</Note>

### Transaction history

After topping up the balance, entries appear in the **Transaction history** table on the plans page:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| **Date** | Date and time of the transaction |
| **Amount** | Top-up amount |
| **Currency** | Payment currency ($ or ₽) |
| **Words** | How many words were added to the balance |
| **Gateway** | Payment gateway — e.g., "International" or the corresponding Russian gateway |
| **Plan** | Which plan was applied (Starter / Pro / Business) |
| **Method** | Payment method (card, transfer, cryptocurrency) |
| **Status** | Successful transaction or error |

### Factors affecting word consumption

| Factor | Impact |
|---|---|
| **AI employee response length** | Longer responses use more words |
| **Conversation context** | Longer dialogs consume more — the AI considers previous messages |
| **[Knowledge Base](/en/guides/knowledge)** | Querying the knowledge base increases usage |
| **[Profiles](/en/guides/profiles) (Realtime)** | Updating the profile after each message adds words |
| **[Talents](/en/guides/talents)** | Using external integrations may increase usage |

## Tips for reducing costs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Shorten response length">
    Add an instruction to the [Persona](/en/guides/persona) tone: "reply briefly, no more than 2-3 sentences." This can cut word usage several times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch profile updates">
    Change Realtime mode to scheduled updates (e.g., once every 24 hours). This significantly reduces consumption.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optimize the knowledge base">
    Remove outdated and redundant documents. The more compact the [base](/en/guides/knowledge), the fewer words are used per query.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use quick replies (FAQ)">
    Set up [FAQ](/en/guides/faq) for common questions — they use fewer resources than full response generation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step-by-step guides

### How to view expenses for a period

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the section">
    Navigate to **Billing History** in the left menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open filters">
    Click the **Filters** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select dates">
    Choose the desired date range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the filter">
    Click **Apply** — all data will recalculate for the selected period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How to find which employee spends the most

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open filters">
    In the **Billing History** section, click **Filters**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an employee">
    Choose a specific AI employee in the employee field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the data">
    Check the summary cards and charts — they will show usage for that specific employee.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare">
    Repeat for another employee and compare results.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How to top up your balance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click 'Top Up'">
    Use the button at the bottom of the sidebar or **"Top Up Balance"** on the billing history page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an amount">
    Select an amount or word package.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the payment">
    Confirm and complete the payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What are 'words' and how are they counted?">
    Words are the unit of measurement on the platform. Every message (from both the client and the AI employee) consumes a certain number of words. This includes the message text, conversation context, [knowledge base](/en/guides/knowledge) queries, and [talent](/en/guides/talents) operations. A typical AI employee message can consume from a few hundred to several thousand words.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the cost per message differ between employees?">
    The cost is affected by response length, context size (length of the previous dialog), [knowledge base](/en/guides/knowledge) and [talent](/en/guides/talents) usage, and the [profile](/en/guides/profiles) update mode. An employee with short responses and scheduled profile updates will cost significantly less.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does the 'Type' column in the table mean?">
    The "Type" column shows the charge category. If no type data is available, "No data" is displayed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if the word balance runs out?">
    All active AI employees will pause. Clients will stop receiving responses until you top up your balance. We recommend topping up in advance and monitoring the progress bar.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between 'Excluding Bonuses' and 'Including Bonuses'?">
    "Excluding Bonuses" shows only paid word usage. "Including Bonuses" also counts words received through the [referral program](/en/guides/referral) or promotions. If you do not participate in bonus programs, there will be no difference.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I view expenses for a specific employee?">
    Yes. Click **Filters** and select the desired AI employee. All data on the page will recalculate for that employee only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How often is the data updated?">
    Data is updated in real time. Every new message is immediately reflected in the statistics.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where can I check my balance without going to Billing History?">
    The balance is always visible at the bottom of the sidebar (dollar amount and word count) and in the top right corner of any page (button with the dollar amount).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which plan should I pick?">
    If you're just launching 1–2 AI employees and want to try the platform — go with **Starter ($100 / 2,000,000 words)**. With steady usage of more than 2M words a month, move to **Pro ($500 / 10,000,000)** — the price per word drops. Chains and agencies with high client traffic pick **Business ($1000 / 20,000,000)**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I pay from Russia?">
    Yes. In the "Russia" region three methods are available: bank transfer, invoice payment, and cryptocurrency. Conversion rate $1 = 100 ₽.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does the 'Gateway' column in the transaction history show?">
    The gateway is the payment system that processed the transaction. For example, "International" if you paid with a foreign card. For Russian payments, the corresponding Russian gateway is shown.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
