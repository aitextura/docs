**Charge History** is the platform's financial hub, where all statistics on word and fund usage are collected. Here you can see how many words each AI Employee used, what it cost, how spending changed by day and month, and manage plans and balance top-ups.

## Page overview

When you open the section, you see several blocks from top to bottom:

| # | Block | Purpose |
|---|------|------------|
| 1 | **Word balance** | Progress bar and usage figures |
| 2 | **Bonus accounting mode** | Toggle between bonus and paid words |
| 3 | **Summary metrics** | Cards with totals: messages, chats, words |
| 4 | **Filters** | Choose a period and an AI Employee |
| 5 | **Analytics** | Four charts |
| 6 | **Charge History table** | Breakdown by day |

---

## Word balance

The balance block is at the top of the page:

| Element | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Words used** | How many words you've used out of the total limit (for example, 1,475,624 of 14,200,000) |
| **Progress bar** | A visual bar with the usage percentage (for example, "90% used") |
| **Top up balance button** | Takes you to add funds |

<Warning>
  When usage approaches 90-95%, it's time to top up your balance. If you run out of words, all AI Employees pause and customers stop getting replies.
</Warning>

The balance is also shown in two places on every page:
- In the **bottom-left corner** of the sidebar -- the amount in dollars and the number of words left
- In the **top-right corner** -- a button with your current balance in dollars

---

## Bonus accounting mode

Below the balance block is a toggle with two tabs:

| Mode | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Excluding bonuses** | Actual spending -- only what was charged to your paid balance |
| **Including bonuses** | Total usage, including words received through bonus programs and promotions |

<Tip>
  **Excluding bonuses** is selected by default. If you take part in the [partner program](/en/guides/referral) or have received bonus words, switch to the second mode to see the full picture.
</Tip>

## Summary metrics

Three cards with key figures for the selected period:

| Metric | What it means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Messages** | Total number of messages and their total cost | 5,308 ($0.0000) |
| **Chats** | Number of unique conversations and their total cost | 424 ($0.000) |
| **Words** | Total number of words used | 12,724,376 |

The heading above the cards shows the period -- **All time** by default. When you apply filters, the figures are recalculated. The **Messages** card is clickable -- click **Details** for a breakdown.

## Filters

The **Filters** button lets you narrow the data by two parameters:

- **By period** -- a specific date range (week, month, or custom interval)
- **By employee** -- spending by a specific AI Employee

<Tip>
  The employee filter is especially useful if you have several AI Employees. It quickly shows which one uses the most resources -- so you can optimize its instructions or [Knowledge Base](/en/guides/knowledge).
</Tip>

After you apply filters, all data on the page -- summary metrics, charts, and the table -- is recalculated.

## Analytics -- four charts

The **Analytics** section contains four charts, available at `/billing/words/history`. You can collapse and expand the block by clicking it.

| # | Chart | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Word and message usage by day** | How many words and messages were used on each day of the period |
| 2 | **Cost by day** | Daily spending in dollars + the maximum and average daily cost |
| 3 | **Chats and messages by day** | Customer activity: the number of new conversations and messages |
| 4 | **Monthly cost trend** | Long-term dynamics -- total spending and average daily cost by month |

### 1. Word and message usage by day

A combined chart with two axes:
- **Left axis** -- number of words (thousands)
- **Right axis** -- number of messages

It shows the ratio of words to messages: some days have few messages but high word usage (long conversations), and vice versa.

### 2. Cost by day

A line chart of spending in dollars. Above the chart you'll see:
- The **maximum** daily cost (for example, $48.89)
- The **average** daily cost (for example, $4.42)

It helps you spot peak days and understand what caused them.

### 3. Chats and messages by day

A combined chart:
- **Left axis** -- number of chats (conversations)
- **Right axis** -- number of messages

It shows customer activity: how many new conversations and messages were handled.

### 4. Monthly cost trend

A long-term chart with two lines:
- **Total spending ($)** -- total spending for each month
- **Average daily spending ($)** -- the averaged daily cost

It shows the overall trend for budget planning.

## Charge History table

A detailed table broken down by day. Each row is one day with charges.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Date** | The charge date (DD.MM.YYYY) |
| **Type** | The charge type (if applicable) |
| **Words** | Number of words for the day |
| **Messages** | Number of messages for the day |
| **Cost** | Total cost for the day in dollars |
| **Conversations** | Number of unique chats for the day |
| **Per conversation** | Average cost of one conversation |
| **Per message** | Average cost of one message |

<Tip>
  Table rows are clickable -- click any row to see a detailed breakdown for that day.
</Tip>

Pagination at the bottom of the table lets you move between pages.

## Plans

The AI Textura platform uses a pay-per-word model:

**Cost = Number of words x Price per word**

The price per word depends on your plan. The bigger the word package, the lower the price per word.

### Current plans

| Plan | Price | Word volume | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | $100 | 2,000,000 words | Getting started, testing, 1–2 AI Employees |
| **Pro** | $500 | 10,000,000 words | Active use, several AI Employees, moderate customer flow |
| **Business** | $1000 | 20,000,000 words | Chains, agencies, high customer flow, or large [Knowledge Bases](/en/guides/knowledge) |

<Frame><img src="/public/screenshots/63-billing-topup.png" alt="Balance top-up page with plans" /></Frame>

<Tip>
  The price per word on the **Business** plan is lower than on **Starter** thanks to the package size. If you consistently use more than 2 million words a month, a larger package is more cost-effective. Check your current usage in the [Charge History table](#charge-history-table) and on the charts.
</Tip>

### Regions and payment methods

The platform supports two regional payment gateways:

| Region | Payment methods |
|---|---|
| **Russia** | Bank transfer (to a settlement account), Pay by invoice (the platform issues an invoice), Cryptocurrency |
| **World** | International cards and payment systems |

The conversion rate for ruble payments: **$1 = ₽100**.

<Note>
  The **Cryptocurrency** method is available in the Russia region -- it's convenient if you don't have Russian bank details or if your card doesn't go through on international gateways.
</Note>

### Transaction history

After you top up your balance, entries appear in the **Transaction history** table on the plans page:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| **Date** | Transaction date and time |
| **Amount** | Top-up amount |
| **Currency** | Payment currency ($ or ₽) |
| **Words** | How many words were added to the balance |
| **Gateway** | Payment gateway -- for example, "International" or the corresponding Russian gateway |
| **Plan** | Which plan was applied (Starter / Pro / Business) |
| **Method** | Payment method (card, transfer, cryptocurrency) |
| **Status** | Successful transaction or error |

### What affects word usage

| Factor | Impact |
|---|---|
| **AI Employee reply length** | The more detailed the reply, the more words |
| **Conversation context** | Long conversations use more -- the AI takes previous messages into account |
| **[Knowledge Base](/en/guides/knowledge)** | Querying the Knowledge Base increases usage |
| **[Profiles](/en/guides/profiles) (Realtime)** | Updating the profile after every message adds words |
| **[Talents](/en/guides/talents)** | Using external integrations can increase usage |

## Tips for reducing spending

<Steps>
  <Step title="Shorten replies">
    Add an instruction to the [Persona](/en/guides/persona) tone: "reply briefly, no more than 2-3 sentences". This can cut word usage several times over.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change how profiles update">
    Replace Realtime mode with scheduled updates (for example, once every 24 hours). This significantly reduces usage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optimize your Knowledge Base">
    Remove outdated and redundant documents. The more compact your [Knowledge Base](/en/guides/knowledge), the fewer words each request uses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use quick answers (FAQ)">
    Set up [FAQ](/en/guides/self-learning) for common questions -- they use fewer resources than generating a full reply.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step-by-step instructions

### How to view spending for a period

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the section">
    Go to **Charge History** in the left menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open filters">
    Click the **Filters** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose dates">
    Specify the date range you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the filter">
    Click **Apply** -- all data is recalculated for the selected period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How to find out which AI Employee spends the most

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open filters">
    In the **Charge History** section, click **Filters**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an AI Employee">
    In the employee field, select a specific AI Employee.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the data">
    Look at the summary metrics and charts -- they show that AI Employee's usage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare">
    Repeat for another AI Employee and compare the results.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How to top up your balance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Top up">
    Use the button at the bottom of the sidebar or **Top up balance** on the charge history page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an amount">
    Select an amount or a word package.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the payment">
    Confirm and complete the payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title='What are "words" and how are they counted?'>
    Words are the platform's unit of accounting. Every message (from both the customer and the AI Employee) uses a certain number of words. This includes the message text, the conversation context, [Knowledge Base](/en/guides/knowledge) queries, and [talent](/en/guides/talents) activity. A typical AI Employee message can use anywhere from a few hundred to a few thousand words.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the cost per message differ between AI Employees?">
    The cost depends on reply length, context size (the length of the previous conversation), use of the [Knowledge Base](/en/guides/knowledge) and [talents](/en/guides/talents), and the [profile](/en/guides/profiles) update mode. An AI Employee with short replies and scheduled profile updates costs significantly less.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does the Type column in the table mean?">
    The Type column shows the charge category. If there's no type data, it shows "No data".
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if my word balance runs out?">
    All active AI Employees pause. Customers stop getting replies until you top up. We recommend topping up in advance and keeping an eye on the progress bar.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between Excluding bonuses and Including bonuses?">
    Excluding bonuses shows only paid word usage. Including bonuses also counts words received through the [partner program](/en/guides/referral) or promotions. If you don't take part in bonus programs, there's no difference.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I view spending for a specific AI Employee?">
    Yes. Click **Filters** and select the AI Employee you need. All data on the page is recalculated for that AI Employee only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How often is the data updated?">
    The data updates in real time. Each new message is reflected in the statistics immediately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where can I see my remaining balance without opening Charge History?">
    The balance is visible at the bottom of the sidebar (amount in dollars and number of words) and in the top-right corner of every page (a button with the amount in dollars).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which plan should I choose?">
    If you're just launching 1–2 AI Employees and want to try the platform, choose **Starter ($100 / 2,000,000 words)**. With steady usage of 2 million words a month or more, move to **Pro ($500 / 10,000,000)** -- the price per word goes down. Chains and agencies with high customer flow choose **Business ($1000 / 20,000,000)**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I pay from Russia?">
    Yes. The Russia region offers three methods: bank transfer, pay by invoice, and cryptocurrency. The conversion rate is $1 = ₽100.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does the Gateway column in the transaction history show?">
    The gateway is the payment system the payment went through. For example, "International" if you paid with a foreign card. For payments from Russia, it shows the corresponding Russian gateway.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I need an invoice and closing documents">
    [Support](/en/guides/support) issues invoices: write the amount you need -- the invoice is sent to your email and duplicated in the chat. Request certificates of completion and other closing documents the same way, specifying the period and email. If you use electronic document exchange (EDO), mention it in the same request.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to sign a contract?">
    No. Payment constitutes acceptance of the [public offer](/en/terms-and-policies/offer); no separate contract is needed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="On the free plan, the AI Employee stopped replying">
    The daily request limit was reached -- the AI Employee stays silent until the next day. Integrations with PMS and spreadsheets also don't work on the free plan: to test them, you need a paid plan or a trial bonus from your manager.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
