Quick Answers are preconfigured responses to specific questions or situations. Instead of "thinking" and composing a reply from scratch every time, the AI Employee instantly performs the action you defined: sends exact text, asks another employee for help, or transfers the customer to another AI Employee.

## When to use them

- Certain questions need a **strictly fixed answer** (address, company details, business hours)
- You need to **automatically transfer** the customer to another AI Employee when a specific topic comes up
- You want to **speed up** handling of common requests without spending tokens on "thinking"

---

## How Quick Answers differ from FAQ

<Warning>
  Quick Answers **always** take priority over FAQ. If a customer's question matches both a quick answer and an FAQ entry, the quick answer fires.
</Warning>

| | Quick Answers | FAQ |
|---|---|---|
| **Who creates them** | You set them up manually | The employee fills them in automatically from conversations |
| **Control** | Full — you define both the question and the action | Partial — the employee decides what to add |
| **Priority** | Higher — checked first | Lower — used if no quick answer fired |
| **Action types** | 5 types: exact text, adaptive answer, transfer, request, expert council | Text answer only |
| **Adaptation** | "Reply exactly" sends the text verbatim; the others adapt | Answers can adapt to context |

<Tip>
  To add ready-made answers manually, use Quick Answers rather than FAQ. FAQ is primarily designed to be filled automatically from conversations.
</Tip>

## 5 action types

### 1. Reply exactly

The employee sends the customer **exactly the text** you specified — unchanged, with no adaptation.

**Good for:** addresses, company details, contact information, legal wording that must not change.

<Note>
  Exact answers don't adapt to the conversation context. The customer gets the same text no matter how they asked. Make sure your answer is worded to fit any case.
</Note>

### 2. Based on answer

The employee uses your text as a **basis** but **adapts** it to the conversation context. You give the employee a "hint," and it builds a reply that accounts for what the conversation was about.

**Good for:** answers that should take context into account (information about a specific product or service), and situations that need flexibility within a guiding frame.

### 3. Transfer to another AI

The customer is **fully handed over** to another AI Employee. The current employee stops working on this conversation, and the other one takes over.

**Good for:** a customer contacting you about another department's topic, or setups where one employee handles first contact and specialized employees do the detailed work.

**Additional settings:**
- **Employee selection** — choose from the dropdown which AI Employee receives the customer
- **Instruction** — text describing the context of the handover

### 4. Get answer from another AI

The current employee **doesn't hand over** the customer; instead, it requests information from another AI Employee **"behind the scenes."** The customer doesn't see this — they keep talking to the same employee.

**Good for:** when you need expertise from another area but transferring the customer isn't appropriate; when one employee is the "face" of the company and others act as internal experts.

**Additional settings:**
- **Employee selection** — who answers "behind the scenes"
- **Instruction** — what to find out from the other employee

<Tip>
  **How is this different from "Transfer to another AI"?** With a transfer, the customer is fully handed over to another employee. With "Get answer," the current employee only requests information and passes it on to the customer itself. The customer never learns that anyone else was involved.
</Tip>

### 5. Expert council

The most powerful option: **2 to 10** AI Employees draft their own answers at the same time, and the system **combines** them into one final message.

**Good for:** complex or ambiguous questions, complaints and conflicts, questions that need a comprehensive approach.

**Additional settings:**
- **Employee selection** — which AI Employees take part in drafting the answer
- **Instruction** — a shared task for all experts
- **Show expert answers** — if enabled, the customer sees each expert's version (by default, only the final combined answer)

## Main question — the trigger

The main question is the trigger the system uses to decide when to run a quick answer. The AI analyzes **meaning**, not exact word matches, so you don't need to write the question word for word the way a customer would.

**Good examples:**
- "What's your address?"
- "How do I request a refund?"
- "I have a technical problem"

**Bad examples:**
- "Question about the address" — too vague
- "The customer asks where we are located, what the office address is, and how to get to us by public transport or by car" — too long

<Tip>
  Phrase the question the way a real customer would ask it. Write from the customer's perspective, not the operator's. The limit is 50 words.
</Tip>

### The "Simplify" button

Next to the **Main question** field there's a **Simplify** button. It runs an AI that rephrases your question in a shorter, more general form.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter a question">
    Type the question in the **Main question** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Simplify">
    The AI suggests a more concise wording.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    If you like the result, keep it. If not, edit it manually.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Example:**
- Before: "Could you please tell me the address of your main office?"
- After: "What's the office address?"

## Additional parameters

### Chat control

A dropdown that defines what happens to the chat after the quick answer fires. For example, you can pause the chat so a manager can step in.

### Send notification

A toggle. When enabled, a manager receives a notification whenever the quick answer fires.

<Warning>
  Enable **Send notification** for quick answers related to complaints, refunds, and other situations that may need a human.
</Warning>

## Step-by-step guides

### How to add a quick answer with exact text

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the tab">
    Go to the **Quick Answers** tab of the employee you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new one">
    Click **+ Add quick answer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the trigger">
    In the **Main question** field, enter the trigger question, for example: "What's your address?". Optionally, click **Simplify**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the action">
    Select **Reply exactly**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the answer">
    In the **Answer** field, enter the exact text the customer will receive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure and save">
    If needed, set **Chat control** and **Send notification**. Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How to set up a transfer to another AI

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a quick answer">
    Click **+ Add quick answer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the trigger">
    In the **Main question** field, enter the trigger, for example: "I have a technical problem".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the action">
    Select **Transfer to another AI**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the employee">
    From the dropdown, select the employee who will receive the customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an instruction and save">
    In the **Instruction** field, describe the handover context. Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How to use Expert council

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a quick answer">
    Click **+ Add quick answer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the trigger">
    In the **Main question** field, enter the trigger, for example: "I want to file a complaint".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the action">
    Select **Expert council**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add experts">
    Add expert employees (2 to 10).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure and save">
    Write a shared instruction for the experts. Decide whether to enable **Show expert answers**. Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Quick Answers priority

When a customer sends a message, the employee checks information sources in a specific order. Quick Answers have **higher priority** than FAQ and the Knowledge Base.

If the employee answers a question differently than you'd like (using FAQ or the Knowledge Base), create a quick answer with the wording you want — it will take priority.

<Tip>
  In the test chat, click the checkmark next to the employee's reply to see its reasoning: which quick answers it checked, what it used from the Knowledge Base, and why it answered the way it did.
</Tip>

## How many to create

There's no limit. Practical recommendations:

- **Don't duplicate similar wording.** If you have a "What's your address?" trigger, you don't need a separate "Where are you located?". Use the **Simplify** button.
- **Don't overload.** Quick Answers are for truly common situations. Let the employee handle rare questions through the Knowledge Base.
- **Review the list regularly.** Delete outdated quick answers — old addresses, prices, expired promotions.

## How it works with other tools

- [FAQ](/en/guides/self-learning) — they complement each other. Quick Answers for critical questions, FAQ for everything else.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How many quick answers can I create?">
    There's no limit. We recommend not duplicating similar wording — use the **Simplify** button to generalize instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if a question matches several quick answers?">
    The system picks the one closest in meaning. Try to phrase triggers so they don't overlap.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is &quot;Get answer from another AI&quot; different from &quot;Transfer to another AI&quot;?">
    With a transfer, the customer is fully handed over to another employee, who continues the conversation. With "Get answer," the current employee requests information from another one "behind the scenes," and the customer doesn't see it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to click Simplify?">
    No. But it's recommended if your trigger question is long or uses specific wording. Simplifying helps the employee recognize different variations of the same question.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I combine Quick Answers with FAQ?">
    Yes. Quick Answers and FAQ complement each other. Quick answers always have higher priority.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A quick answer isn't firing — what should I do?">
    Check the trigger wording — it may be too narrow or too broad. Try clicking **Simplify** and saving again. Test it in the test chat and review the employee's reasoning (the checkmark next to the reply).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use Quick Answers without a Knowledge Base?">
    Yes. The "Reply exactly" action doesn't depend on the Knowledge Base. But for the "Based on answer" action, a Knowledge Base significantly improves answer quality.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
